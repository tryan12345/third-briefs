THIRD. · Brotege · Strategy

Brotege × TikTok Shop US

Since Third took over the account in May, Brotege has done $6.5K in total shop GMV. There are enough creators under the 20,000-follower cap. The gains are in reach per video, repeat posting and the gifting buyer, with Night Train launching in early October.

01 · The position

Creators post Brotege once. Few of their videos reach anyone.

Since Third took over (May 1 to September 29, 2026), Brotege sold 283 units for $6,534.47 in total shop GMV, almost all of it Good Boy Protection Daily Moisturizer (SPF 15 and retinol, $28.50 list). Night Train, a nighttime moisturizer for men at $34.99, launches in early October.

The finding

Brotege's shortfall sits in two places: videos that never reach 500 views, and creators who post once and stop. Both are fixable inside the 20,000-follower cap.

Views are what matter. Since May, an affiliate video with 2,000 or more views sold 83% of the time and one with 500 to 2,000 views sold 24% of the time. Under 500 views, it sold 2.4% of the time. Affiliates who posted three or more videos earned $35.80 each; one-video affiliates earned $3.64.

02 · Trajectory

GMV fell by a third after June as video output stayed low

Total shop GMV averaged $1,617 a month in May and June and $1,100 a month from July to September. The average week dropped from $260 in the four weeks from June 29 to $189 in the four weeks to September 27.

The 17 new videos in the last two weeks of July were followed by two of the three best weeks of the quarter. Recent weeks keep collecting GMV for several weeks, so September will move up somewhat.

03 · Diagnosis

What is working and what is not, since May

Working

  • Gifting hooks sell best. "Men, women, both, stop. Pay attention to this," followed by "Do you have a boyfriend? A husband?", sold 17.8 units per 1,000 views in July. A mom buying for her son sold 12.8 in August.
  • Women sell a men's product. 9 of the 13 affiliates who converted since May are women, and 11 of the 13 have a mostly female audience.
  • Videos that get seen, sell. 5 of the 6 affiliate videos that passed 2,000 views made a sale.
  • Follower count does not sort winners. Creators with 5,000 to 10,000 followers sold most often (20% of their videos), ahead of creators over 20,000 (15.4%), so the cap costs little.
  • Price and social proof hooks. "Who else ordered tons of Good Boy Protection because today's offer was too good to pass up?" sold 2.9 units per 1,000 views.

Not working

  • Reach. 60% of affiliate videos posted since May have under 500 views, and those sold 2.4% of the time.
  • One-and-done creators. 57 of the 82 affiliates who posted since May posted one video. One-video affiliates sold 8.8% of the time; affiliates with three or more sold 35.7% of the time.
  • Product shows up late. Videos with the product on screen in the first three seconds sold 20% of the time, against 4.8% when it came later (96 analyzed videos).
  • Sit-down reviews and demos. Videos showing Good Boy used inside a routine sold 38.1% of the time (21 videos). Sit-down reviews sold 10.6% (47) and demos 6.3% (16).
  • GMV leans on brand posts. Brotege's own account earned $1,441 on 49 videos since May, against $822 for all 141 affiliate videos combined.

Standing rules

  • Cap: 2,000 target plans a week, only to creators under 20,000 followers.
  • Order: priority lists first, in the order of the workbook, then the full prospect pool by GMV on like products.
  • Ask: three videos per creator, the first within 14 days of the sample landing. In October: two Good Boy, one Night Train.
  • Brief: product on screen in the first three seconds, shown in use inside a routine, opening with a hook from section 08.
  • Spark: ad spend on any creator video that passes 500 views in its first 72 hours. Every plan asks for the Spark code.
  • Email: runs alongside target plans to every list creator with a public email, with no volume target. It is judged on replies and samples requested.
  • Terms: Good Boy open plan stays at 15%. Good Boy and Night Train sell as separate SKUs.

11 · Risks and data notes

What could change this read

GMV bases

Total shop GMV and all benchmark figures are marketplace estimates of gross TikTok-attributed GMV. Affiliate GMV, video counts, videos per affiliate and retention come from Brotege's shop performance data, counted on videos posted since May 1, 2026, when Third took over, unless marked all time. Brotege's own brand-account videos are excluded from affiliate figures. Each figure is labelled with its base.

Commission at 15%

Scaled products in the band pay 20% open. At 15%, target plan acceptance may run lower than the model's base case. Week 4 data will show it.

Acceptance rate unknown

The model uses 2%, 4% and 6%. At 6% Brotege would ship about 520 samples a month, so the sample budget may cap invites.

Small samples

The format and first-three-seconds findings rest on 96 to 109 analyzed videos posted since May. They are directional.

Lagging credit

Shop data credits some recent videos late. The top gifting hook shows $0 in shop data but $428 in marketplace data.

Creator lists

List GMV is lifetime GMV on like products, from marketplace data. 28-day activity was not available per creator, so a creator can rank high on past sales and be quiet now; confirm recent posting before sampling. Gender comes from each creator's display name, handle, bio or Brotege's shop data, and should be confirmed before outreach.

Ad-served GMV

Ad-served share shows paid traffic touched a video. It does not show how much of the GMV the ads created.
