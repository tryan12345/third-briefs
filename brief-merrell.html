For creators filming the Moab 3 as the official shop opens into the fall and Black Friday windows.

Provisional

The house bar for a full-confidence brief is 75 analyzed videos and 1,500+ mined comments. It gets rebuilt on first-party trading data after the shop opens.

2 · Content angles

A · The fall outfit loop

Seven to twelve seconds. The boot styled into one outfit, on body, walking. Caption names the colorway and the season. This format carries the top ten videos on the comparable boot and it is built to be boosted.

Objection: "What do I wear it with" · Archetype: Outfit Looper

B · The deal page

Price, availability, and the window, stated plainly over the product. The top three sellers on the $230 comparable boot all run this format at volume. Use it when launch pricing and Black Friday deals are live.

Objection: "I'll get it later" · Archetype: Deal-Page Stacker

C · The Spanish-first styler

The outfit loop or gift framing in Spanish. Spanish-language versions converted at five times the English loops per view in the comparable pull, and almost no one makes them.

Objection: "Is this worth gifting" · Archetype: Multilingual Styler

D · The full-day verdict

Your size, the size you took, and how the boot felt after a real day on your feet. Short. Reviews show she trusts a worn verdict and warns others about sizing unprompted.

Objection: "Do I size up" and "Is it comfortable" · Archetype: Comfort Verdict

E · The water test, supporting

Water hitting the shoe, then the dry sock. The August data showed this converts, and it backs up the Waterproof and GTX variants. Run it behind the styling angles, with the fit statement included.

Objection: "Is the waterproofing real" · Archetype: Trail Demonstrator, supporting bench
