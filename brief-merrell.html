01 · The winning formula

Non-negotiables

Every rule below comes from a video that sold. The reference set is the top converting outdoor and comfort footwear on TikTok Shop US, 30 days ending August 14, 2026.

Do

  • Open on the water hitting the shoe. Not on your face, not on the box.
  • Keep it between 10 and 40 seconds. Every top performer in the set sits in that range.
  • Say your normal size and the size you are wearing out loud, in the first 15 seconds.
  • Shoot somewhere ordinary. A creek on a normal walk, a wet parking lot, a rainy morning.
  • Say the price. The buyer is comparing to a $62 boot whether you mention it or not.
  • Show the shoe after the water. Wet on the outside, dry inside, sock off.

Don't

  • Don't read the spec sheet on camera. Vibram and Kinetic Fit belong at the end, as backup.
  • Don't shoot a summit. The buyer is walking a trail on a Saturday.
  • Don't say waterproof without showing water. The claim is the demonstration.
  • Don't open with an unboxing. The claim comes before the reveal.
  • Don't use a trending sound that buries the audio of the water.
  • Don't skip sizing. Every unanswered sizing question turns into a return.
02 · Content angles

Four angles, each aimed at a real objection

Angle 01

The water test

Answers: is it actually waterproof or just water resistant.

Walk into moving water. Hold. Come out. Pull the shoe off and show a dry sock.

Proof · the highest reach video in this category is exactly this. 920,600 views, $7,019.
Angle 02

The all-day count

Answers: can I wear these when I am not hiking.

Timestamped day. Trail, then errands, then evening, then the next morning. Same shoe throughout.

Proof · bestselling hiking apparel on the platform sells on comfort and everyday wear, not performance.
Angle 03

The sizing verdict

Answers: do I size up, and what about wide feet.

Straight to camera. Your normal size, the size you ordered, how it fits with a thick sock.

Proof · an 11 second sandal video returned $13.47 per thousand views doing exactly this.
Angle 04 · The reply

Reply to a comment as the video premise. Answer the question plainly and demonstrate while you talk. This produced the highest GMV per thousand views in the entire reference set, $20.93, on a 0.13% interaction rate. It also solves the perennial problem of what to post next, because the comments write the brief for you.

05 · Objections to answer on camera

Say it before the comments do

Pick two per video. Trying to cover all six turns a 20 second demonstration into a two minute review, and the review does not sell.

Fit and sizing

  • Do I order my normal size, and what if I am between sizes
  • Do these work for wide feet
  • How do they feel out of the box, before any break-in

Claim and price

  • Is it waterproof or water resistant, and for how long
  • Can I wear these all day if I am not hiking
  • Why is this worth $150 when there is a $62 waterproof boot on the same page
06 · Specs & logistics

Shooting and posting

Shoot

  • Length: 10 to 40 seconds. The four reference winners run 10, 11, 21 and 37.
  • Setting: a real creek, a wet parking lot, a rainy morning. Not a studio, not a summit.
  • Format: vertical 9:16, 1080x1920 minimum. Shot on a phone.
  • Audio: your own voice over ambient sound. Keep the water audible.
  • Required shots: water hitting the shoe, the walk through, the shoe coming off, the dry sock.
  • Hook window: first 2 seconds. The demonstration starts immediately, with no intro or greeting.

Post

  • Link: Moab 3 Waterproof unless a promo brief says otherwise.
  • Tags: tag the Merrell product in the video before posting.
  • Lighting: daylight. Overcast is fine and suits the wet weather angle.
  • Cadence: the creators who earn most in this category post weekly across a full season, not once.
  • Comments: answer sizing questions in the first 24 hours. Those replies are your next video.
  • Questions: go to your Third account lead.
