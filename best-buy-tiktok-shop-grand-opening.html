Commitments

To qualify

  • A1 P0/P1 seller at GMV tier T0 to T3. The GMV tier is waived for a first brand campaign.
  • A TikTok or Instagram account with 100K+ followers
  • Enrollment in the Smart Promotion Basic Package

Hero products and pricing

  • At least 5 of the top 10 bestsellers across all channels as hero products
  • 70% of hero products at P5/P6 on TikTok Shop. All hero products SKPP qualified.
  • Lowest price across all platforms for the past 6 months and the campaign, matching allowed. Or at least 1 TikTok Shop exclusive benefit, such as a launch, gift, spec or packaging.

Business results

  • $70K+ incremental GMV over 7 days against a baseline 3 weeks before launch
  • $50K+ incremental ad spend from Best Buy's own budget. Ad credits do not count.
  • All brand ads link inside TikTok Shop. No links to bestbuy.com or other retailers.
