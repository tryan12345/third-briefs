TikTok Shop Year 1 comparables

What the first year looks like

GMV ramp, creator scale and hero products for seven retail and brand shops, measured from the day each shop opened. Built to give Target leadership a frame for the Q1 forecast and the first 12 months.

Bottom line

Small first month, fast months two and three

Six of the seven shops did under $200K in their first 30 days. The ramp started in months two and three. The strongest shops reached $1.6M to $3.5M by day 90.

Year 1 GMV landed at 7x to 16x the day 90 number for every shop with a full year of history.

The three fastest ramps had 3,700 to 4,500 affiliate creators by day 90. The fastest single start, Sephora, came from one hero product.

Growth drivers

What moved the curve

Launch timing

Medicube, Crocs, QVC and Gap opened between May and August. Their first six months ran into Q4. Crocs did about $7.5M in November and December alone.

A January launch gets no holiday lift in the first half of the year. Ulta opened in March and is the closest timing match.

Hero concentration

The fastest starts leaned on a few products. Sephora's opening weeks are 90% one product. Medicube's top skincare set accounts for 17% of the shop's lifetime GMV.

QVC, Ulta and Bullseye Deals spread sales across hundreds or thousands of SKUs. No single product passed 5% of GMV at any of the three.

Top product share of shop GMV

Percent of total shop GMV from the single best-selling product

Creator scale

Medicube, Crocs and QVC each had 3,700 to 4,500 affiliate creators by day 90. Bullseye Deals had 150 and stayed flat until it added creators in the back half of its first year.

Ulta had about 4,600 creators by day 90 and still did $290K. Creator count alone did not produce the ramp. The shops that combined creator scale with a short hero list moved fastest.

Activation model

QVC ran a LIVE-led launch with about 25,000 affiliate LIVEs in Year 1. Gap ran a video-led launch with about 26,000 videos and 1,500 LIVEs. Both models reached Year 1 scale.

For Target's plan

Reading the comps against a January launch

Ulta is the timing comp. It opened outside of Q4 and did $290K in its first 90 days with a broad assortment.

Sephora is the model comp. A short hero list with creator activity timed to launch produced the fastest start in this set. Target's 12 hero items with creator videos ready at launch follow that model.

A reasonable frame for leadership is a first 90 days closer to Ulta's timing, with upside toward the faster ramps if the hero items catch and creator scale builds behind them. The holiday step the 2024 launches saw would arrive in months 11 and 12.

Marketing investment

Affiliate creator count is the clearest investment signal available for these shops. Outside estimates of paid media spend are not reliable enough to put in front of leadership. We can walk through spend ranges live.

How to read this

GMV figures are estimates of public shop sales, rounded. Day counts run from the date each shop opened.

Product GMV is lifetime to date for products first listed in each shop's opening year. For Ulta and Sephora, lifetime and Year 1 are the same.

Creator counts are affiliate creators linked to the shop. Crocs shows a small restatement around month 10, so its curve uses quarterly points.
