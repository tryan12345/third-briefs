TikTok Shop Year 1 comparables

What the first year looks like

Monthly GMV, creator scale, paid media and hero products for seven retail and brand shops, measured from the day each shop opened. Built to give Target leadership a frame for the Q1 forecast and the first 12 months.

Bottom line

Small first month, fast months two and three

Six of the seven shops did under $200K in their first 30 days. The ramp started in months two and three. The strongest shops reached $1.6M to $3.5M by day 90, and Year 1 landed at 7x to 16x that number.

At a 2 to 3 ROI on total GMV, the scaled shops put 33% to 50% of GMV into paid media. Crocs-type scale implies $11M to $16M in paid during Year 1. The shops with light paid support are a fraction of their size.

Marketing investment

Paid media at the comps

Spend here assumes a 2 to 3 ROI on total GMV. At a 2 ROI, $1M in GMV takes $500K in paid. At a 3 ROI, it takes $333K.

The scaled shops run half or more of their GMV through ads. At that ROI they put 33% to 50% of GMV into paid. Medicube spends about $1.7M to $2.6M a week. QVC spends about $790K to $1.2M and Crocs about $650K to $970K.

Sephora is spending at the same level from launch. That's about $390K to $580K a week against about $1.2M a week in GMV.

Ulta, Gap and Bullseye Deals run light paid support. Only 5% to 24% of their GMV comes through ads. Their spend is shown as ad-driven GMV at the same ROI, which puts them at 2% to 12% of GMV. All three sell a fraction of what the scaled shops do.

What this means for the forecast

A forecast that looks like Crocs or QVC assumes $9M to $16M in paid during Year 1, or 33% to 50% of GMV. A forecast with light paid support looks more like Ulta and Gap.

Growth drivers

What moved the curve

Launch timing

Medicube, Crocs, QVC and Gap opened between May and August. Their first six months ran into Q4. Crocs did about $7.5M in November and December alone.

A January launch gets no holiday lift in the first half of the year. Ulta opened in March and is the closest timing match.

Hero concentration

The fastest starts leaned on a few products. Sephora's opening weeks are 90% one product. Medicube's top skincare set accounts for 17% of the shop's lifetime GMV.

QVC, Ulta and Bullseye Deals spread sales across hundreds or thousands of SKUs. No single product passed 5% of GMV at any of the three.

Top product share of shop GMV

Percent of total shop GMV from the single best-selling product

Sale events

Platform sale weeks drove the biggest single-month steps at Ulta, Crocs and QVC. Ulta's July event week was about 60% of its July GMV.

Activation model

QVC ran a LIVE-led launch with about 25,000 affiliate LIVEs in Year 1. Gap ran a video-led launch with about 26,000 videos and 1,500 LIVEs. Both models reached Year 1 scale.

What must be true

Conditions behind the faster ramps

A short hero list

The fastest starts put 10% to 90% of GMV on a single product.

Creator scale by day 90

About 4,000 affiliate creators by day 90, with steady growth after.

Paid media behind it

Paid media at 33% to 50% of GMV once the shop is scaling, at a 2 to 3 ROI, with half or more of GMV running through ads.

An event calendar

Hero inventory and creator content ready for each platform sale week.

For Target's plan

Reading the comps against a January launch

Ulta is the timing comp. It opened outside of Q4 and did about $300K in its first 90 days with a broad assortment and light paid support.

Sephora is the model comp. A short hero list with creator activity and paid media timed to launch produced the fastest start in this set. Target's 12 hero items with creator videos ready at launch follow that model.

A reasonable frame for leadership is a first 90 days closer to Ulta's timing, with upside toward the faster ramps if the hero items catch and paid investment scales behind them. The holiday step the 2024 launches saw would arrive in months 11 and 12.

How to read this

GMV figures are estimates of public shop sales, rounded. Months are 30-day periods counted from the date each shop opened. Crocs uses calendar-month steps.

Crocs shows a small GMV restatement around month 10. Its months 9 and 10 are shown as an even split of the two-month total.

Creator counts are affiliate creators linked to the shop. Paid media assumes a 2 to 3 ROI on total GMV for Medicube, Crocs, QVC and Sephora. Ulta, Gap and Bullseye Deals run light paid support, so their spend uses the same ROI on ad-driven GMV only.

Product GMV is lifetime to date for products first listed in each shop's opening year. For Ulta and Sephora, lifetime and Year 1 are the same.
