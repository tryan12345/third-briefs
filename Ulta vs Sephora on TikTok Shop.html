Bottom line

Sephora's first drop weekend did $36.6K on TikTok Shop. Ulta did $39.8K in its whole first month on the platform. Ulta still sold $92K on the same weekend Sephora launched, and it runs about $612K every 28 days.

Ulta runs a traditional shop. It has 740 SKUs, over 10,000 linked creators and brand lives several times a week. Sephora runs one exclusive drop a month built around a single mega live. Both are pulling from the same pool of top beauty commerce creators, and at least one of them is selling for both.

The launch

Sephora's first three days outsold Ulta's first month. Ulta took under $3K in its first three days and $39.8K in its first 28. Sephora passed $36K by day three.

Most of the gap comes from the format. The Sephora Drop Shop features one partner brand a month. Teasers and creator content build to an official TikTok LIVE with a celebrity host, the brand founder and creators. Drop products sell only on Sephora's TikTok Shop first. Some roll out to Sephora.com and stores later. Ulta launched March 17 with a curated set of only-at-Ulta brands and grew into a full catalog.

Ulta's always-on shop still runs on tentpoles. Non-event days in August ran $4K to $7K. 21 Days of Beauty started on TikTok Shop Aug 29 and pushed most days to $15K to $50K. July 22 to 27 did $363K. That is a quarter of Ulta's $1.45M over the last 90 days.

Creators

Sephora used a small group of top sellers around the drop. Ulta has a long tail of mid-tier creators selling every day.

Sephora's drop roster is made up of proven sellers. Lifetime GMV: Alle Brean $27.2M, Kelly Kruger $11.4M, Jeselle $5.1M, Kristi Dmytrenko $4.3M, Erika Hayes $2.6M, Mustbecindy $2.3M and Jodie $1.9M. Their drop videos each sold one or two vaults on 135 to 3,659 plays. Every top Sephora video is flagged as paid. 305 of 422 videos went up on Sept 19 and 20. 102 creators linked on Sept 20 alone. Outside Sephora's own live, the other 188 creators produced about $3.3K.

Ulta has 10,400 video creators and 383 live creators. The top video sellers are mid-tier:

  • Alexis Leianna, 37.6K followers, $14.1K
  • Dani (@blitzypicks), 9.4K followers, $9.9K, mostly Spanish-language Anua videos
  • Lucky Lynndee, Amy Lynn and Kaylee round out the top five

The 10K to 50K follower tier drives the most Ulta GMV at $101K from 1,494 creators. The 100K+ tier did $95K from 320 creators. Paul (@paulreactss) posted Ulta's top video at 996K plays and $5.1K on a Squishmallows fragrance.

Blue Waters sells for both. That account has $32.6M in lifetime GMV. It ran Ulta lives on Sept 5 ($9.4K) and Sept 18 ($4.3K), then Sephora's debut creator live on Sept 20 ($3.7K). It was the same host with the same audience. The first Ulta live converted at $348 per 1K entries. The Sephora live converted at $150.

Video efficiency

Ulta earns $41.84 per 1K views and $29 per video. Sephora sits at $2.14 per 1K views and $19 per video.

One Sephora video skews the number. Sephora's own paid lip tint video took 1.6M of the shop's 3.7M plays and returned $1,246. Without it, Sephora is at $3.18 per 1K views. Most Sephora videos are under 48 hours old, so this will climb.

Sephora's videos promote the live. Ulta's videos sell product directly. 9 of Ulta's top 10 videos are flagged as paid.

Merchandising

Ulta sells a broad catalog of value sets priced $15 to $45. Sephora sells one exclusive high-ticket kit plus low-priced private label.

Ulta carries 740 active SKUs. Makeup is 48% of GMV and skincare is 14%. Fragrance is 24% of GMV from only 6.5% of SKUs. Bath and body is 9%. 57% of GMV sits between $15 and $45.

Where they overlap

The two shops have a lot in common.

  • Their own account is the top seller in both shops.
  • Both lean on paid amplification. 9 of Ulta's top 10 videos and all 10 of Sephora's are flagged as ads.
  • Both use exclusives as the hook. Ulta leads with only-at-Ulta brands. Sephora leads with a TikTok-first drop.
  • Both draw from the same creator pool, mostly women 25 to 44. Blue Waters is already selling for both.
  • Both build around live events. Ulta runs them weekly and Sephora runs one a month.
