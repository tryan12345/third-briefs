The headline

Thirty L3+ creators to approach, a live-only offer for each show, and three steps to get them live

The SOW asks for at least one L3+ creator live a week. These 30 creators sold $1.5M on live across all brands in the last 28 days, and 13 of them have sold Tonies on live. Every one of them either sells toys on live or has sold Tonies on live. A Tonies show can be a full live or a Tonies segment inside a show the creator already runs.

The 30 is an outreach list, not a minimum. There is no set number of creators we need to go live with. We expect about 20% to agree to feature Tonies, which is about six creators from 30. A creator who does one Tonies live between now and the end of December is a win.

Creators identified for outreach, with no minimum to go live: 8 referred by our TikTok account manager and 22 from our own Top 100 Live list.

At 12% to 15%, a creator earns $25.20 to $31.50 per Pooh bundle. The flash deal and bundle run only during the live. Exclusivity is the reason a viewer buys in that creator's live instead of later. Each creator gets her own live-only deal or bundle for her show date.

Paying creators

Work unpaid first: commission, the live-only offer and the product. If a creator asks for a fee, Client Success reviews the remaining budget to see what we can allocate, likely $100 an hour. Big-box hosts such as beckotto487 are the most likely to ask for one.

Step 3 · Delivery · Outreach and live creator management

Reach the 30 with the offer, lock their dates and get them the products

  • Outreach. Delivery contacts all 30 creators in one wave with the live-only offer, the lead product and a proposed date and format (full show or Tonies segment). If response is low, keep going down the Top 100 Live tab of the tonies creator lists sheet.
  • Black Friday support. Justin (Client Success) locks the eight referred creators into their Black Friday lives with TikTok. This runs separately from the weekly outreach.
  • Lock in. Confirm the date, start time, slot length and format. Hand any fee request to Client Success.
  • Live schedule. Delivery builds one shared calendar of creator lives so no two creator lives overlap, and keeps them clear of the tonies.us brand live times.
  • Merchandising setup. Send each show's products and offer to Shop Operations at least 48 hours before the live, so the promo and bundle are ready.
  • Creator comms. Send each creator the products to show on the live, with a target collab at the higher live commission, so they earn the live rate on every sale.
