02 · The archetypes

Four creator types sell outdoor footwear

Weight the roster toward the first two. The second pair covers the slip-on and style lanes and carries less GMV per video.

Archetype 1

The Demonstrator

$7.63 / 1K

Puts the claim through a physical test on camera. Walks into water. Stands in a hose. Wears them twelve hours and reports back. Highest reach in the set.

Proof · @im_thaliaaa, 445,229 followers, 2,197 videos. One 21 second creek video took $7,019 on 920,600 views.
Use for · Moab 3 Waterproof and GTX
Archetype 2

The Utility Seller

$20.93 / 1K

Posts constantly in a plain format. Frequently replies to a commenter's question as the video premise. Often Spanish language. Best efficiency in the set.

Proof · @santianariba, 37 second reply covering comfort, weight, slip resistance and toe protection. $4,939 on 236,000 views.
Use for · Moab 3, work adjacent SKUs
Archetype 3

The Style Poster

$4.08 / 1K

Ten seconds, on feet, seasonal framing. Outdoor product treated as a fashion object. High engagement, weaker as a pure sales unit.

Proof · @twiedekind, 26,677 followers, 2,410 videos. A Timberland fall styling video at 2.25% interaction, $742 on 182,000 views.
Use for · Alpine 83, Wrapt, 1TRL, Lonely Ghost
Archetype 4 · The Comfort Reviewer

Short verdict videos. Names the fit, names the feel, confirms sizing without being asked. @greenreaper.xo, 35,692 followers, 1,737 videos, returned $13.47 per thousand views on an 11 second sandal video at a 0.36% interaction rate. Use for Hydro Moc and Jungle Moc against Crocs and HeyDude. This archetype is the sizing answer, so brief them to state it every time.

Sourcing rules

  • Pull from existing rosters. NORTIV 8, WateLves and Timberland creators have already sold outdoor footwear and do not need to be taught the category.
  • Filter on recent posting, not followers. A creator with 1,788 followers and a posting habit outperforms one with 366,481 and no habit.
  • Recruit Spanish language creators deliberately. The highest earning creator in outdoor footwear works in Spanish and this lane is uncontested.
  • Size the sampling plan first. Outreach and approval throughput is the constraint on every program we have run.

What not to screen on

  • Interaction rate. The best converting creator in the set runs 0.13%. Screening on engagement removes the utility sellers.
  • Follower floors. A 5,000 follower minimum would have excluded a creator who drove $69,794.
  • Outdoor niche only. The converting roster is deals and finds accounts, not hiking accounts.
  • Polish. Studio quality output underperforms phone footage in this category.
