The headline

Reach out to 30 L3+ creators and lock in whoever says yes, with a goal of 3 to 5 Tonies lives a week from Dec 1 to Dec 31

The SOW asks for at least one L3+ creator live a week. These 30 creators sold $1.2M on live across all brands in the last 28 days, and 10 of them have sold Tonies on live. Every one of them either sells toys on live or has sold Tonies on live. Each creator is asked for a Tonies segment inside a live she already runs, not a full Tonies show.

The 30 is an outreach list, not a number to lock in. Delivery reaches out to all 30 and books any creator who agrees. The goal is 3 to 5 creators going live with Tonies each week, Dec 1 to Dec 31. We expect about 20% to agree, about six creators from 30, which covers the goal if most of them go live weekly.

All creator lives are unpaid: the creator earns the live commission and keeps the product.

Step 2 · Client Success · Lock the merchandising and commission

Confirm December deals and live commission with Justin before any target plan goes out

Justin owns the merchandising. The deals Tonies runs in December are what creators offer on their lives. There is no extra flash sale on top of those deals. What creators can get is a live commission.

What to confirm with Justin

  • The December deals for each week, and which products they cover.
  • The live commission rate for creator lives.

Once both are confirmed, Delivery knows what to put in each live creator’s target plan.

Step 3 · Delivery · Outreach and live creator management

Reach all 30 with the offer, lock in whoever says yes and get them the products

  • Outreach. Once Justin confirms the December deals and live commission, Delivery sends the 30 live creators target plans with that deal and commission. Depending on appetite, Delivery keeps reaching out to more creators on the Top 100 Live tab of the tonies creator lists sheet.
  • Black Friday support. Justin (Client Success) locks in the eight referred creators with Shirley, our TikTok account manager. This runs separately from the outreach to the 30.
  • Lock in. Keep it flexible. Each creator only needs to tell us the date she is going live and the Tonies product she will feature, so the merchandising can be set up. No live schedule to build.
  • Merchandising setup. Confirm with Shop Operations at least 48 hours before each live that the December deal is live on the products she will show.
  • Creator comms. Send each creator the products to show on the live, with a target collab at the live commission, so they earn it on every sale.
