01 · The winning formula

Non-negotiables

Four things appear in every Velvetiser video that cleared $3,000. Miss one and the video underperforms.

Do

  • Show the full make, start to finish. Milk in, flakes in, whisk running, pour. No cuts that skip the process.
  • Land the pour shot. Slow, thick, frothy, into the glass. This is the conversion moment in every top video.
  • Say "real chocolate flakes, not powder." The single most repeated proof point across winners.
  • Show the box contents. Machine, base, hot glass, cold glass, 10-serving variety pack. Buyers do not know what they get.
  • Name the flavors out loud. Salted caramel, hazelnut, vanilla white, mint, Black Forest, creme brulee. Flavor lists drive comments.
  • Handle the machine. Pick it up. It reads heavier and more substantial than it looks in photos.
  • Address heat directly. Say it comes out at the right temperature and ready to drink.

Don't

  • Don't open on the brand name. "Hotel Chocolat" means nothing to a US scroller. Open on the outcome or the problem.
  • Don't call it a hot chocolate machine first. Against packets, $212 is absurd. Frame it as a countertop appliance.
  • Don't skip the price. Videos that named a stacked discount converted above 12%. Hiding the price does not help.
  • Don't sell it as winter-only. It makes cold chocolate and ships with a cold glass.
  • Don't show a half-filled or thin pour. Thin liquid reads as expensive powdered cocoa.
  • Don't stack it with the chocolate boxes. Different buyer, different price point. Separate videos.
  • Don't overclaim the size of a serving. It is single-serve. Say so before the comments do.
02 · Content angles

Five angles, each aimed at a real objection

Angle 01

The impossible person

For the person who already has everything. This is the highest-revenue angle on record and it produced the two top Velvetiser videos of November 2025.

Answers: "will they actually like it"
Best for: Archetype A, October through December
Angle 02

Flakes, not powder

Open on a packet of instant cocoa, cut to the flakes going in. Explain the vortex whisk and the ridged interior. This is the price justification.

Answers: "$212 for hot chocolate?"
Best for: Archetype A, year-round
Angle 03

Iced chocolate for summer

Lead with the cold drink. It ships with a cold glass. Two July 2026 videos on this angle converted at 12.6% and 15.2%, the highest rates in the 2026 dataset. Post this in a concentrated push, not a slow drip.

Answers: "will I use it after February"
Best for: Archetype A, April through September
Angle 04

Where it came from

Own cacao farms in Ghana and Saint Lucia. Gentle farming. Designed by chocolatiers with a ceramic artist to mimic a chocolatier's pot. This gives the buyer permission to spend.

Answers: "is this just an expensive gadget"
Best for: Archetype A, gifting season
Angle 05

Stacked discount

Sale plus coupon plus free shipping, with a deadline. Short and hard. Reserve for real price events. Every top-converting deal video in the set names the exact stack.

Answers: "should I wait"
Best for: Archetype B, promo windows only
Angle to test

Buyers report using their own chocolate powder in the machine. That answers the biggest unspoken fear about proprietary refills and nobody has said it on camera. Worth a test video from one Archetype A creator.

04 · Product cheat sheet

The #1 buyer question, answered

The question that decides the sale

"Why is this $212 when hot chocolate is $4 a box?"

Because it is not making hot chocolate from powder. It heats and vortex-whisks real chocolate flakes into a drink you would otherwise pay for at a cafe. It also makes iced chocolate, ships with a hot glass and a cold glass, and the chocolate comes from farms the company owns in Ghana and Saint Lucia. Price it against an espresso machine, not against cocoa.

05 · Objections to answer on camera

Say it before the comments do

These come from actual buyer reviews on the hero SKU. Each one has a line that handles it.

What buyers sayHow to handle it on camera
"It barely gets warm."Show the pour and say the temperature is right and ready to drink immediately. Start with cold milk from the fridge and let the machine run the full cycle. Do not cut the cycle short in the edit.
"It makes a small amount for the price."Name it. Single serve, made fresh, the way you would order it. Compare it to a $7 cafe drink rather than to a mug of powder.
"What happens when the chocolate runs out?"Refills are sold separately in 10-serving boxes, and buyers report using their own chocolate in it too. Do not leave this unanswered.
"Which one do I buy?"Say the SKU name out loud and point at the linked one. Do not list all the colorways and bundles.
"Will I use it after winter?"Show the cold glass and make an iced chocolate in the same video. It ships in the box.
"Is it real chocolate or fancy powder?"Show the flakes in your hand before they go in. This is the strongest single visual in the whole product.
"Does it look cheap in person?"Pick it up on camera. Buyers consistently say it is heavier and larger than it appears in photos.
06 · Specs & logistics

Shooting and posting

Shoot

  • Length: 30 to 60 seconds. Top performers ran 45 to 55.
  • Setting: a real kitchen counter with natural light. Not a desk, not a studio.
  • Format: vertical 9:16, 1080x1920 minimum.
  • Audio: your own voice. Every top-earning video is voiceover, not trending audio.
  • Required shots: flakes in hand, milk pouring in, whisk running, the slow pour, the first sip reaction.
  • Hook window: first 3 seconds. Product visible by second 2.

Post

  • Link: the $212 hero SKU unless a promo brief says otherwise.
  • Disclosure: #hcpartner is required on every post.
  • Tags: @Hotel Chocolat USA in the caption.
  • Hashtags: #hotelchocolat #velvetiser #hotchocolatemaker plus one seasonal tag.
  • Cadence: the creators who earn most on this product post it weekly across a full season, not once.
  • Going LIVE: brew on camera and take temperature and size questions in real time. This is the highest-value format on a $212 appliance.
  • Comments: answer temperature and serving-size questions in the first 24 hours. They convert.
