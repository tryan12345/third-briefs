The finding

The Neck Lift Stick earns about five times more per view than the Body Stick, and only 68 creators have ever sold it.

Neck Lift Stick videos posted in the last 90 days earned $30.31 in GMV per 1,000 views. Body Stick videos earned $6.14. The closest competitor at the same price, Dr.Melaxin’s $17.60 neck cream, has been sold by 787 creators.

The Body Stick still matters. It carries 1,369 active subscriptions and $45.0K of subscription GMV in the window.

03 · Diagnosis

The Neck Lift Stick converts and faces sell. Approved samples are not turning into videos

Working

  • The neck stick converts. 58% of the 393 neck stick videos posted in the window sold at least once. For the Body Stick it was 23% of 882.
  • Faces sell. 36 videos with a face on camera produced $48.7K, 37% of video GMV from the window. They earned $21.97 per 1,000 views against $7.19 for faceless videos.
  • Personal stories on the neck. A creator who lost weight and talks about her own neck took $26.1K on the stick across seven videos.
  • Deal framing for older audiences. Coupon-led neck videos from deal creators with 45 to 55+ audiences sell about 3 to 10 units per 1,000 views.
  • Subscriptions on the Body Stick. 2,348 new subscriptions in 90 days, with 1,369 active now.

Not working

  • Approved samples are under-posting. 479 approved samples produced 306 posted videos, 0.64 per sample. The house benchmark is about 1.5. 67 approved creators are overdue on their video. Set up CRMs and workflows, and follow up with the overdue creators now.
  • Price is carried by discounts. The neck stick lists at $17 and realized $9.49 per unit. The Body Stick lists at $20 and realized $13.86. Get content that drives full-price purchases.
  • The two largest affiliates have stopped posting INKEY. @jopickz posted 81 INKEY videos in August, 19 in September and none since. @justjamiemichelle went from 11 videos and $7.1K in August to 4 videos and $290 in October, while still posting for other brands. Together they hold $208.8K in lifetime INKEY GMV. Re-engage them.
  • Thin reviews. The neck stick has no reviews. The Body Stick has 15. Shop rating is 4.3, down from 4.5 in August. Set up a Seller Center review campaign with an incentive to post a review.

09 · Risks and data notes

What could change this read

Discount dependency

Both sticks are realizing well under list. The best week of the 13 followed a price cut on the neck stick to $12.70. If Black Friday pricing goes deeper, units rise and GMV per unit falls. Deal-led creators convert best on the neck stick, so a price change after the peak will show up in their numbers first.

Neck stick inventory

About six weeks of cover at current pace, before any Black Friday lift or paid spend. A stockout would stop the creator pipeline we are building.

Concentration

Ten videos produced 53% of video GMV. New creators take 2 to 6 weeks to show up in GMV, since that is how long a sample takes to become a video.

Thin review base

No neck stick reviews and 15 Body Stick reviews. The buyer persona and brief lean on competitor reviews and on what selling videos say, and are marked provisional.

Two GMV bases

Shop figures are INKEY’s own shop data at realized prices. Market figures are marketplace GMV at listed price, about 1.5 times higher for INKEY on the same units. All GMV is gross TikTok-attributed GMV, not net revenue.
