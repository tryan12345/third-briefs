The number one question

"Which mop am I actually getting?"

O-Cedar sells three similar systems at three prices: EasyWring at $51.99, H2prO at $49.50 to $58.50, and RinseClean at $64.99 and up. Comparison videos are a top-selling format because buyers cannot tell the systems apart from the listings. The one-star record includes buyers who ordered RinseClean and believe they received EasyWring, or an older version without separated tanks. Answer the question before she asks it, on camera and in the cart.
