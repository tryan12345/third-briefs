THIRD. · Merrell · Strategy

Merrell × TikTok Shop US

Merrell has $3,096 of gray-market GMV on TikTok Shop and no shop to receive it. No outdoor footwear brand runs a managed presence. The lane is open.

01 · The position

Demand exists and there is nothing to receive it

People search Merrell on TikTok Shop and buy. The orders go to resellers. There is no Merrell shop, no affiliate roster, and no control over price or sizing information.

Twenty-plus separate reseller listings carry the Merrell name. Several show a $0.00 price, which means the listing is broken and the buyer sees nothing. The same Moab 3 appears at $97.95, $141.35, $151.51, $170.00 and $173.00 depending on which seller the buyer lands on.

The Lonely Ghost x Merrell sweat shorts did $2,903 on 55 units at $53.10. One video, 5,561 views, produced $106 of it. The other $2,797 came through the product card. Apparel currently outsells every Merrell shoe on the platform combined.

The read

Merrell already has search demand on TikTok Shop. It has no store to point that demand at, so a reseller collects it and sets the price.

The only brand entity registered under the Merrell name is "MerrellCD," which has $0 GMV and is filed under Phones & Electronics. Columbia does $121 a month unmanaged. Salomon does not appear in the brand registry at all. No hiking or outdoor specialist appears in the top 25 shoe brands.

Why this matters for Merrell

Opening a shop converts the search demand on day one. That is the 69.5% line, and it is available without any content. It is also where Timberland stopped. Everything above that number has to be built.

What the category rewards

  • Demonstrable claims. Waterproofing shown on camera, not stated in a caption.
  • Creator count in the thousands. Crocs runs 18,481 creators a month.
  • Price between $45 and $140. Proven at volume across clogs, boots and sneakers.
  • Small creators posting constantly. Follower count does not predict GMV here.
  • Comfort framing for women, work framing for men. Both convert.

What it does not

  • Spec language. Nobody buys a midsole compound on this platform.
  • A shop with no affiliate program. Timberland has 1,272 creators and $37K a month.
  • Summit imagery as the default setting. The buyer is walking, not summiting.
  • Reseller pricing. $88 to $174 for the same shoe tells the buyer nothing is official.
05 · Where the revenue would come from

The mix Merrell should be aiming to invert

Timberland's channel split is what a heritage outdoor brand gets by default on this platform. It is also the thing a program is supposed to change.

The product card share falls as a percentage even while it grows in absolute terms. That is the correct shape. A shop where product card share stays above two thirds after six months has a roster problem, not a demand problem.

The number to hold the plan to

Observed GMV per video in this category runs from $44.52 at Timberland to $612.24 on NORTIV 8's best SKU. At the house cold start rate of 50 videos per week, that range brackets roughly $24,000 to $132,000 a month once the roster is posting. The spread is wide because it depends entirely on creative discipline, which is what the brief exists to control.

06 · The plan

Four moves, in this order

The sequence matters. Product card revenue is available on day one and requires no content. Everything after that is built.

Move 01 · Weeks 1 to 4

Open the shop and consolidate the catalog

The search demand already converts. Timberland gets 69.5% of its revenue this way and Merrell currently gets none of it. This also ends the $88 to $174 price spread and the broken $0.00 listings.

Move 02 · Weeks 2 to 12

Build the roster against the waterproof demonstration

Moab 3 Waterproof is the hero. House cold start target is 50 videos per week, moving to 2,000 per month once the formula holds. Sampling throughput is the constraint. Creator supply is not.

Move 03 · Weeks 6 to 16

Run Hydro Moc into the Crocs lane

Different creator set, different buyer, same shop. It gives the roster a sub-$100 entry product while Moab carries margin.

Move 04 · Week 12 onward

Hold paid media until the content base exists

GMV Max needs roughly 50 videos per hero product or 600 shop videos before it has anything to optimize against. Timberland ran 1,066 live sessions in 90 days for $1,134.

08 · Risks

What could break this

Catalog control

Merrell needs to confirm whether it can enforce takedowns on the existing reseller listings, or whether the official shop runs alongside them. Twenty-plus listings currently price the same Moab 3 between $97.95 and $173.00. That spread undercuts every price statement a creator makes on camera.

Sampling capacity

Footwear sampling carries size variance, which raises cost per sample against a typical consumable and complicates returns. The monthly sample ceiling sets the entire video forecast and is not yet known. Every program we have run is constrained here rather than on creator supply.

Seasonality against the launch window

The waterproof demonstration is strongest in wet and cold weather. A launch that lands after the autumn window loses the most persuasive version of the hero creative and has to carry the shop on the slip-on lane instead.

No first-party data

Every figure in this document is marketplace evidence and gray-market signal. FastMoss returns zero reviews across the reference set, so there is no mined buyer language behind the persona work. The first 60 days of shop data should be treated as the real baseline and these assumptions revisited against it.
