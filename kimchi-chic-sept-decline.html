What the list shows

  • Fourteen creators are fully dark. itstesatiara and kingkayyy_c_ are still posting. itstesatiara is ahead of her summer pace. kingkayyy_c_ slowed from about 4.4 videos per 15 days to 3. Neither one's September posts have made real GMV yet.
  • Lexi is the biggest gap. She's an L5 doing about $200K a month across TikTok Shop. Five videos made us $16.7K. She's about $1.9K behind run-rate after two weeks.
  • Rachel Marie and Natalie stopped completely. They posted 27 videos between them in July and August and none in September. Rachel's August videos still did $1.6K in September.
  • Summer hits from bentlylovesyou, findzzfordays, artistzone2 and aggayucita each did $1.3K to $3.2K. They're decaying and nobody has posted a replacement.

LIVE

LIVE GMV fell from $5,844 to $2,082, down 64%. That's about a quarter of the total gap.

mariyaaaaa23 drove $13.9K of the shop's $17.9K LIVE GMV in July and August, 78% of it, across 21 lives. She's done 3 lives and $863 in September. The shop's LIVE spikes stop after Aug 13. That lines up with when total GMV broke.

Other things tied to the decline

Ad spend

Spend was $13.0K vs $14.6K, down 11%. ROI held at 9.03 vs 9.08. GMV fell 12%. Spend and GMV moved almost one to one, same as the one-pager.

  • Daily spend dropped on Aug 3, from about $1,070 a day in July to under $1,000.
  • Shop Tab GMV and product impressions both fell 12.7%.
  • Blended cost per order went from $2.36 to $2.47 on shop-wide orders.

ROI is at 9x. There's room to push spend back to July levels.

Sample approvals

  • Requests were up 53%, 1,369 vs 897, on an 80% increase in DMs.
  • Approvals were down 27%, 268 vs 365. The approval rate dropped from 41% to 20%.
  • Deliveries fell to 218 in late August, down from 376 in early August. With a 2 to 6 week lag, that hit September posting.
  • At 1.5 videos per approved sample, September approvals point to about 400 videos landing in October.

Content velocity

Posting was 1,742 videos in July and 1,542 in August. September is pacing at about 1,250. Our benchmark for a performing brand is 2,000 or more a month.

Category

FastMoss shows US Beauty units down about 7% for Sep 1–15 vs Aug 16–31. KimChi Chic units are down about 5% over the same window. Since mid-August the shop is roughly tracking a softer category. The drop from early August is on the shop.

Channel mix

FastMoss, last 28 days: video is 57% of GMV, product card is 40% and LIVE is 3%. Video and product card account for most of the gap.

Where I'd push

  1. Move ad spend back toward $1,070 a day and watch ROI.
  2. Re-seed the dark creators with new samples and a new angle. Lexi first, then Rachel Marie and Natalie.
  3. Find out why Mariya stopped going live. Get her back to about 3 lives a week or add a second host.
  4. Review the sample approval filter. Requests are coming in and approvals are holding them up.
Cruva attribution lags on recent windows, so September GMV per video will move up as those videos age. Cruva and FastMoss GMV totals differ on recent dates and track closer on older ones.
