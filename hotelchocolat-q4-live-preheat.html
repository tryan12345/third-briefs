Q4 TikTok Shop LIVE preheat strategy

Hotel Chocolat

How we build and optimize preheat creative for 75 hours of LIVE, from the first show on October 19 through the last-minute gifting MegaLive on December 10. Prepared by Third for Hotel Chocolat. September 2026.

The headline

Thirty preheat assets carry the Q4 LIVE calendar across two layers.

The program starts October 19 and runs 75 LIVE hours. An evergreen creative engine supports the recurring LIVE calendar. Dedicated tentpole bursts build audiences for the First LIVE, Black Friday / Cyber Monday and two holiday MegaLives.

The goal of the evergreen layer is to find the strongest format, hook and reason to register. A single winning video is a byproduct of that.

1. The evergreen preheat engine

Three formats launch together through GMV Max ahead of the first LIVE.

Each video tests a meaningfully different creative format. Three variations of one execution would tell us about the edit. Three formats tell us what makes people register.

GMV Max Live performs with 3 to 6 preheat pieces in rotation. Three evergreen assets plus the tentpole preheats keep us inside that range.

Preheat 01

Product and gifting

Lead with hero SKUs, gifting occasions, bundles or a compelling LIVE offer.

Preheat 02

Personality-led invitation

Personality-forward creative that invites viewers to the upcoming LIVE and teases what they can expect.

Preheat 03

Curiosity and FOMO

Lead with exclusivity, limited-time pricing, gifting finds, product demonstrations or another strong reason to tune in.

2. Evergreen optimization cadence

A two-week read decides what stays, what goes and what we make next.

Evergreen launches 7 to 10 days before the first LIVE. If the October 19 date moves, every date in this section moves with it.

Assumption: the Nov 6 to 9 and Nov 20 to 23 windows apply the two-week loop to the calendar. Adjust to match real launch dates.

Every read ends in one of three decisions.

KeepStrongest format(s) keep running
CutPause creative not driving registrations
Iterate1 to 2 new preheats on the winner

We do not need new evergreen preheats for every LIVE. The December holiday refresh carries the evergreen pool through the rest of the month. New evergreen work is only needed if the program continues into January.

3. The tentpole preheat model

Each tentpole gets a dedicated 10-day preheat window.

TimingAssetJob
T-10Preheat #1Announcement and registration.
T-5Preheat #2Why to watch: product, gifting or offer.
T-1 to T-2Preheat #3Urgency and countdown.
LIVE day1 to 2 rapid-turn ads"We're LIVE right now" ads that drive viewers straight into the stream.

That is 5 unique assets per tentpole. BFCM runs two shows, so its LIVE day carries 3 to 4 rapid-turn ads.

LIVE-day rapid-turn creative uses what is working in the stream.

For priority LIVEs, the SV Producer is on set capturing content for paid amplification while the show is running. The producer reacts in real time. If a bundle, demonstration, offer or host moment is landing, that becomes the ad.

Core structure

  • Open on "We're LIVE right now."
  • Show the product, host or LIVE energy.
  • Tease the offer or reason to watch.
  • Close on tap to join the LIVE.

The two MegaLives work as one holiday campaign.

MegaLive #1 (Dec 6) and MegaLive #2 (Dec 10) are four days apart, and their preheat windows overlap. MegaLive #1 creative establishes the broader holiday gifting moment. MegaLive #2 creative shifts to last-minute gifting and urgency. Each event keeps a distinct reason to watch without duplicating production.

6. How we will know it is working

Registration efficiency drives the evergreen decisions. GPM and CPO judge the shows.

Evergreen read metricCost per registration
LIVE GPM floor$4
LIVE GPM goalAbove $6
Paid efficiencyCPO

LIVE GPM is GMV per 1,000 viewer entries to the LIVE room. CPO (cost per order) is the primary paid metric for TikTok Shop.

Leading indicators at each read

  • Registrations and cost per registration by format and hook.
  • Hook retention in the first two seconds of each preheat.
  • Frequency and falling click-through as fatigue signals.
  • LIVE room entries sourced from rapid-turn ads during each tentpole.

Risks worth naming

The First LIVE date is not final.

The evergreen launch, the first read and the refresh are all keyed to Oct 19. A move of a few days shifts the whole October cadence.

The approval window before launch is tight.

The first assets go live Oct 9, nine days from today. Brief sign-off this week keeps that date.

BFCM and the MegaLives overlap from Nov 26 to Dec 10.

Up to three preheat campaigns run at once. Clear messaging per event and paid budget split by event keep them from competing for the same audience.

LIVE-day ads depend on a same-day workflow.

Rapid-turn ads only work if edit, approval and launch happen during the show. Pre-approving the ad structure and offer language removes the approval step on the day.
