TikTok Shop Category Opportunity Analysis

Six snacking categories sized and ranked, and a six-month read on the two shops Mars Wrigley already operates.

Prepared for Mars Wrigley  ·  February to July 2026, US  ·  Sources: FastMoss + Cruva  ·  August 4, 2026

1 · The finding

Mars Wrigley has already proven it can sell here, then let it go

Mars operates two shops on TikTok Shop US. Both are running at a fraction of what they did in March, and neither is paying a commission.

Mars Snacking and Hotel Chocolat USA together did $264,847 over the six months from February through July 2026. That is 0.103% of the $257.2M that moved through Food and Beverages in the same window. March alone accounted for 58.6% of the six-month total across both shops. Since then both have fallen off a cliff.

This is not a question of whether Mars can sell on the platform. Five discontinued Mars SKUs have real lifetime GMV behind them, including a freeze-dried M&M'S product that did $131,232. The question is why a company with proven sellers is running seven active listings, zero commission, and no owned content.

The M&M'S Pop'd result is the one to look at. It is a freeze-dried candy at $11.99 and it is the highest-unit product Mars has ever run on this platform at 10,584 units. 4 · The pattern this fits

National CPG brands spike and fade here. Platform-native brands compound.

Mars is not an outlier. Frito-Lay ran $718,157 in Snacks in February, growing 4,659% year over year. By May they had dropped out of the category top ten, and they were still out in July. The tenth-place shop in May did $246,440, so the fall was at least 66%.

Creator video fell from 66.2% of Snacks GMV in March to 46.7% in July, a 19.5 point drop in five months. Live nearly doubled over the same period. Concentration fell too. The top 50 products held 35.5% of Snacks units in March and 27.9% in July. The category is fragmenting, which is the condition an entrant wants.

6 · Category detail

What the product-level data shows

1. Chocolate & Sweets

8.60
$6.93M/mo · 730 SKUs over $1K · 215 over $5K · 28 over $25K · momentum 1.20

Two nodes: Chocolate and Chocolate Snacks, and Candy. Almost all the money is in Candy, which has 637 products over $1,000 led by a Sour Kings bundle at $286,635 and Shameless Snacks at $163,025. The winning format is freeze-dried and sour, in variety boxes at $10 to $50, with commission from 12% to 50%.

Chocolate itself is thin. Only two products clear $25,000 in 28 days and the leader is a protein candy bar at $48,494. But it is running at 1.40 against its 90-day average, the second strongest reading in the set.

This is the one category where Mars has receipts. M&M'S Pop'd, TWIX Cookie Collection, and Skittles Gummies each did over $100,000 lifetime. Dove is still selling today at $23,495 over 90 days with no commission and no content.

2. Savory Snacks

6.20
$3.74M/mo · 246 SKUs over $1K · 81 over $5K · 20 over $25K · momentum 1.60

Strongest momentum in the set at 1.60 and the second largest category. Drizzilicious leads with mini rice cakes at $320,937 on a single 6-pack, and holds two more slots in the top ten. Papá Nicho's Carne Seca and El Ranchito Jerky take the rest.

This is where Frito-Lay tried and faded. It is also where the most durable independent operators live, and El Ranchito has grown in every month sampled.

Mars scores a 3 on right to play. Combos is the only asset and it is not a platform-shaped product. Two listings here look like paid or self-operated spikes, covered in section 8.

3. Gum & Mints

6.10
$977K/mo · 107 SKUs over $1K · 30 over $5K · 4 over $25K · momentum 0.80

Functional oral care has taken the category. The number one product is a remineralizing nano-hydroxyapatite gum at $255,759 over 28 days, selling for $36.80 to $45.00 with 6,757 linked creators and 10,992 videos. Three of the top four SKUs are dental gums.

It is also the weakest momentum reading in the set at 0.80, and that is driven by the leader. Its most recent month is running well below its own 90-day pace. The category is softening, which cuts both ways for an entrant.

Extra, Orbit, Eclipse, and Altoids give Mars a real claim on oral health, and the $37 price ceiling is proven. Neither Mars shop has ever listed a single gum product.

4. Fruit, Nut, Energy & Protein Bars

6.00
$2.22M/mo · 227 SKUs over $1K · 82 over $5K · 19 over $25K · momentum 1.07

Built Bar holds six of the top ten SKUs, with Prime Bites taking two and Barebells one. Built carried 19,720 linked creators in May. Their lead SKU alone has 10,733 creators and 8,782 videos.

The category sells on protein and collagen, not fruit and nut. Every leading SKU is a 12-count box between $29.99 and $44.44. KIND and Nature's Bakery would be entering against a claim they do not currently make.

Deepest creator supply in the set at roughly 29,000 across the top ten. Worst whitespace at 3.

5. Biscuits, Cookies & Wafers

5.60
$661K/mo · 119 SKUs over $1K · 29 over $5K · 2 over $25K · momentum 0.94

Flattest category here. The leader is a Chocbox Dubai chocolate kit at $32,803 and nothing else clears $28,000. No brand has taken it, which is why whitespace scores an 8.

Mars proof is partial. The TWIX Cookie Collection did $130,543 lifetime, which is a cookie product in everything but its category assignment. That is the only relevant evidence.

National brands appear here only through curators. SnackWorks sells an OREO and Sour Patch Kids pack, and it is the fifth-largest listing in the node.

6. Popcorn & Snack Pastries

4.90
$799K/mo · 132 SKUs over $1K · 30 over $5K · 3 over $25K · momentum 1.11

Two nodes combined and neither works for a confectioner. Popcorn is the smallest node measured anywhere in this report, with 19 products over $1,000 and nothing over $25,000. The leader is a Farmer Jon's pack sold by QVC at $12,452.

Snack Cakes and Pastries is larger but the top sellers are Asian snack boxes and mystery boxes. Tribeca Curations runs a Hostess and Little Debbie mystery box at $23,471.

Mars owns nothing in either node. Ranked last on right to play and last overall.

Price is the constraint that shows up in every node. Winning SKUs sell between $20 and $45 as multipacks, variety boxes, and 12-count cases. Mars Snacking already knows this: 100% of its 90-day GMV sat in the $15 to $30 band. The two Mars SKUs that moved the most units were $11.99 each. A checkout-lane price point has no path here, and the commission has to be funded against the basket.

8 · Method, pressure test, and limits

What is measured, what is modeled, and what did not survive

Window

Shop performance covers 1 February to 31 July 2026 at daily resolution, which is the full range FastMoss returns. Category totals cover the same six months at monthly resolution. Product-level competitive reads use trailing 28-day and 90-day figures, which are the longest per-product windows the platform exposes.

The GMV model

FastMoss reports category GMV at level 1 only. For each level-3 node we counted products clearing $1,000, $5,000, and $25,000 in trailing 28-day GMV, then fitted a log-uniform distribution across those bands with the observed category maximum as the ceiling. The model covers products doing $1,000 or more in 28 days, so every figure is a floor. Summed across the six categories it returns 37% of reported Food and Beverages GMV, against Snacks accounting for 42% of category units. Those two numbers sitting close is the check that it holds.

Pressure test

The Mars shop identification was checked from both directions. A shop search on the parent company name returns nothing, which is what produced the wrong answer in the first pass of this analysis. Searching the operating names returns both shops immediately. Separately, 28 Mars-branded listings exist under unaffiliated resellers such as CandyLucky 1990s and Snack Bliss Zone, all reporting zero GMV, zero creators, and zero videos. Those are noise, not Mars.

Two listings in Savory Snacks look like paid or self-operated spikes rather than durable competition. A DOBYONE product reports $200,715 over 28 days under the title "Carefully packaged, promptly shipped, please contact customer service" with zero creators and zero videos, and a duplicate jerky listing from Project Torque reports $152,038 with the same profile. Both are excluded from the competitive read and inflate Savory's count over $25,000 by two out of twenty. A third, EdgeJetty550, held second place in Snacks in February at $1,137,379 on a single product with zero linked creators and a zero shop rating, then vanished.

Known limits

  • FastMoss accepts a 90-day GMV filter on product search but does not apply it. Every threshold returns the same capped row count, so a 90-day category size model is not possible. Category sizes therefore rest on a 28-day base even though the rest of this report runs on six months.
  • Product search caps result counts at 2,000, so any band wide enough to exceed that returns an unusable number. All bands used here sit below the cap.
  • Category sizes are modeled, not reported. Every input is measured, but the totals are estimates.
  • Cruva could not validate at level 3. Its category tree stops at Snacks, so there is no second system reading the same window at the same depth.
  • Mints have no node in the taxonomy and sit inside Candy. Gum and Mints understates the real Mars opportunity and Chocolate and Sweets overstates its own by the same amount.
  • Popcorn and Snack Cakes and Pastries were combined to match the requested category set. They behave differently and should be split before any decision on either.
  • Savory Snacks maps to Crisps and Puffed Snacks. Nuts, Seeds, Seaweed, and Dried Snacks sit in separate nodes and are not counted.
  • March is inflated across the whole category by pre-Easter buying, with Easter falling on 5 April 2026. Category GMV peaked at $49.9M in March against a six-month average of $42.9M. Read the Mars March spike against that backdrop rather than as a campaign result.
  • Frito-Lay's decline is bounded, not measured. They are absent from the May and July top ten, so the fall is at least 66%, but the exact figure was not pulled.

9 · Definitions

TermWhat it means here
GMVGross merchandise value. Dollar value of goods sold through the platform before returns, fees, or commission.
ASPAverage selling price. GMV divided by units sold.
Level 1, 2, 3TikTok Shop's category tree. Food and Beverages is level 1, Snacks is level 2, Candy is level 3.
Linked creatorsCreators who have added a product to their storefront and are eligible to earn commission on it. A proxy for affiliate roster size.
Product cardSales from the listing itself through search, the shop tab, or the product page. No creator and no ad involved.
Shop-owned contentGMV from video or live posted by the brand's own account rather than an affiliate.
Seller depthNumber of products clearing a GMV threshold in 28 days. Measures how many real competitors exist rather than how big the biggest is.
MomentumTrailing 28-day GMV annualized to 90 days, divided by actual trailing 90-day GMV, across the top ten products.
WhitespaceRoom for a new entrant. High whitespace means few products are winning and no brand has locked the category across months.
Club-pack unitA multipack, variety box, or case-count SKU built to hit $20 to $45 rather than a single-serve retail price.
FastMossThird-party analytics tracking total shop and product GMV across TikTok Shop.
