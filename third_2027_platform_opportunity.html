How platforms lift the segments

Platforms send brands to the partners they trust. Every one of those brands is a lead for a segment team. The more work we do with a platform, the more brands it sends us.

It works the other way too. Every enterprise proposal should include a platform plan. Every platform conversation should include the brands we can bring.
