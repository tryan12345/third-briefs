4. Email outreach

Email the 80 Top 100 Affiliate creators who match Tonies

Email has not been used on this shop yet. A creator is a match if she has sold $1K or more of Tonies, or if toys are at least 20% of her sales. That covers 51 past and current Tonies sellers and 29 net-new creators. The other 20 sell mostly outside toys and are left out. Each creator is marked in the Email outreach column of the Top 100 Affiliate tab.

Starting Nov 11, each creator gets a warm, personal email inviting her to request a Tonies sample and post about it, paired with a target collab invite in TikTok Shop. One follow-up goes three days later.

What to replicate

  • Gift framing for a toddler, with the Toniebox 2 bundle in hand in the first second.
  • A real reason to buy now: the Black Friday price, a free gift or limited stock. Only claim what is true on the day.
  • Tell viewers to tap the cart and check their price or coupon.
  • New character releases unboxed with the audio playing on the box.
  • Toniebox 1 versus 2 for parents deciding which to buy.

These hooks make up the one-pager sent with every approved sample. The full creator brief builds on them.
