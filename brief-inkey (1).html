1. Winning formula

Name the problem in your first line

The goal is more creators selling the neck stick through Black Friday and into January. The videos that sold most open on the problem in plain words: “turkey neck”, “crepe”, “neck and chest.” 58% of neck stick videos sold at least once, against 23% for the Body Stick.

DoSay what bothered you about your neck in the first sentence.
Don’tOpen with the brand name or the ingredient.

Show your face and your own neck

Across INKEY videos in the last 90 days, face-on-camera videos earned $21.97 per 1,000 views. Faceless videos earned $7.19. Six of the ten top-selling neck stick videos show the creator.

DoFilm yourself, in daylight, with your neck in frame.
Don’tUse stock footage, a filter or a model.

Tell your story before you show the product

The top creator on the stick opens with her weight loss and took $26.1K from seven videos. Another opens with “thousands of dollars and probably hundreds of products later” and sold 764 units from one video.

DoSay what you tried before and why you kept looking.
Don’tRead the product listing to camera.

Put the stick on in upward strokes, on camera

A reply video explaining the upward motion sold 128 units from 28,287 views. Buyers want to see how it is used on the neck and chest.

DoGlide it from the base of your neck up to your jaw while you talk.
Don’tHold the stick without using it.

Give your own timeframe

Buyers of competing neck creams leave bad reviews when results are slower than they expected: “3 weeks already don’t really see any difference.” Happy buyers say “give it 30 days.”

DoSay how long you have used it and what you see.
Don’tPromise overnight results or a lift that lasts.

If there is a deal, call it early

Deal-led neck videos sold up to 8.9 units per 1,000 views, the highest in the set. The shop’s best week came right after the stick’s price dropped to $12.70 on October 25, and most videos posted that week were coupon-led. Prices change, so the strongest ones send viewers to check their coupons.

DoTell viewers to check their coupons, in the first five seconds.
Don’tQuote a price as permanent.

2. Content angles

Angle 1

My neck, my story

Gap it answers: Trigger 1 (a change she can name) and trigger 2 (loose skin after weight loss). Answers “Is it just lotion?” with your own result.

Archetype: A1, The Neck Storyteller.

How to run it: 40 to 60 seconds, face on camera. Your story, your neck in close-up, the stick going on, your timeframe. Reply to questions in the comments with a new video.

Angle 2

Check your coupons

Gap it answers: Trigger 3, a deal she might miss. Reaches the buyer who was not looking for a neck product.

Archetype: A2, The Deal Caller.

How to run it: 10 to 40 seconds. Stick in hand in the first frame, “check your coupons” in the first line, then one sentence on what it does and one on how you use it.

Angle 3

Why I switched from my neck cream

Gap it answers: Trigger 1 and the format benefit. Most neck buyers already own a jar. Answers “Is it just lotion?” by showing the difference in use.

Archetype: A3, The Neck Category Switcher.

How to run it: Show the cream you used before. Say what you did not like about it. Show the stick going on with no mess and say what it costs next to it.

Angle 4

Crepey neck, sensitive skin

Gap it answers: The irritation objection. Buyers who have reacted to retinol or acids need to hear from someone like them.

Archetype: A1, The Neck Storyteller.

How to run it: Say your skin is sensitive and what has bothered it before. Say you patch tested and where. Show how your skin looked after a week.

5. Objection questions to answer on camera

Answer these out loud. Do not wait for the comments.

  • “Is it just lotion?” Say what the listing says it contains and show your own result over time.
  • “How long until I see a difference?” Give the number of days or weeks you have used it. Do not promise a result for anyone else.
  • “Does it smell?” Say what it smells like to you. Scent is a common reason buyers return neck creams.
  • “Will it irritate my sensitive skin?” Say you patch tested and where, and how your skin reacted.
  • “Is this retinol?” The listing names Ceramide NP. Check the full ingredient list on your pack before you answer.
  • “Will the price go back up?” Say what you paid and tell viewers to check their coupons. Do not promise a price.
