A · What moved it

Growth peaked with every lever on. Pulling back in July slowed sales, and we've reactivated levers with a focus on profitability.

Feb to Apr · Creators scaled

  • Creators posting rose from 244 to 331, and videos from 535 to 885.
  • About 320 samples shipped each month, two-thirds of them on best sellers.
  • One video sold $41.1K of the Foldover Skirt in March.

May to Jun · Levers on

  • FBM joined Deals for You Days, and ad spend rose to $33.6K while ROI improved from 3.11x to 3.51x.
  • With creators, campaigns and spend all working together, sales per video rose from $139 to $238, and conversion rose from 0.77% in April to 1.37% in June.
  • Cozy Crush Short grew from $29.6K to $52.0K.

Jul to Sep · Levers pulled back

  • After a profitability review, we opted out of Smart Promotions and TikTok Shop campaigns.
  • Ad spend was cut in half, to $17.5K, and the Shop Ads commission dropped to 10%.
  • The free-shipping threshold moved to $50, so the Cozy Crush Short no longer qualified.

Oct to Nov · Rebuilding

  • Enrolled in Smart Promotions, and free shipping is back to $30.
  • Sampling volume is up in October and November.
  • The Softie Seamless Long Sleeve restock became a top seller.
  • Daze Off launched October 22 and took off quickly.
  • November is on pace for a record month.

B · What's working

Creators sell, Millie mentions convert, and best sellers pay back every sample.

Affiliate

  • Creators sell the shop. 79% of 2026 sales ($907.7K) came from creator videos. 876 creators have made at least 1 sale in 2026.
  • Top creator retention stays strong. The top 10 creators drive 42% of sales, and all 10 still post today.
  • Sampling is back up. About 450 samples went out in October, with about 500 slated for November.
  • Contests drive revenue. The spring challenge paid $1,950 in prizes to 5 creators, who drove $56.4K in sales from 131 videos. The winner drove $23.1K from 41 videos. Next: a creator contest every month or two.

Millie, merchandising and heroes

  • Millie content converts. Top video hooks open with "Thanks Millie, selling out again."
  • New colors in best-selling silhouettes work. Fall colors lifted the Seamless Long Sleeve about 600% in August. The Foldover Shortie rose 255% after restock.
  • Best sellers pay back per sample and more. July to September, each sample of the four main best sellers drove about $456. Cozy Crush drove about $740 per sample and the Cotton Tee about $410.
  • Creators want the best sellers. Two of every three August sample requests were for best sellers.
  • Two best sellers were sampled every month. Cozy Crush Sweat Short and the Cotton Tee, FBM's #1 and #2 products.

C · What's not working

Most of the Q3 decline came from choices we have already begun reversing.

Campaigns, merchandising and paid media

  • Removed from campaigns. No Flash Sales, Weekly Promos or monthly campaigns from July to September. Next: maintain Smart Promotions and campaign enrollment in 2027.
  • New launches sold less than heroes, and in-stock heroes were under-sampled. About 200 samples went to the Take Me Home and Classic Boxer & Cami Sets and the Barrel Leg and Wide Leg Sweatpants. Creators posted (70% to 89% post rates), but the videos sold $2.4K. Next: seed new launches big and fast, read the first 2 to 3 weeks, then scale or stop.
  • Proven best sellers were left out. The Skirt, Full Length Tank and Shortie got 47 samples from July to September and still sold $59K. Next: 60% of samples go to best sellers every month.
  • The hero product lost free shipping. Moving the minimum to $50 put the $39.95 Cozy Crush under the line. Its sales fell 83% from June to September. Next: keep the free shipping minimum at $30.
  • Holiday-heavy sampling in November. 228 samples were approved by November 9, with the focus on holiday. Next: expand sampling to heroes to avoid losing momentum once holiday sells through.
  • Paid media was cut in half. Fewer people saw FBM. Product impressions fell 39% in August to 5.4M, and reached 3.8M in October. Next: optimize ROI and ad spend campaigns.

Creators, LIVE and inventory

  • Creator LIVEs sold nothing. 32 creators went live with FBM in their showcase in 90 days and sold $0. FBM was one of 50 to 100 products. Next: build a live creator strategy and scale.
  • Big creators are missing. Our new top 100 list surfaced 35 strong matches we haven't captured yet, 7 of them in the top 25. Comfrt's sweatpant has 3,625 creators. Cozy Crush has 369, even with a higher commission. Next: focus here, with a flat fee plus commission for the top 10.
  • Low inventory paused momentum on top sellers. When the Pointelle Set and Cozy Crush sizes ran out, their samples went to new launches instead of other in-stock best sellers. Next: plan inventory in advance for hero SKUs, with a contingency plan for samples.

09 · Paid media and inventory

Put ad dollars and stock behind the products that already sell

Paid media

  • The creator halo effect. Put spend behind FBM's best TikTok creator videos on Meta to drive traffic to your .com.
  • Give best sellers their own campaigns. Separate the heroes from the rest of the catalog so budget goes to what converts, instead of spreading across the entire catalog.
  • Raise spend on heroes and new colors. Back each color drop, the Daze Off sleep line and top creator videos as they go live.
  • Hold spend when sales dip. In 2026, ad spend fell 48% from June to September along with sales. In 2027, spend stays at 15% to 20% of monthly GMV (20% max) to lift the slow months.

Inventory: plan hero stock ahead, so it doesn't sell out

  • Plan hero stock a season ahead. Both Foldovers sold out in April. The Pointelle Set was out until August 26. Cozy Crush was missing small sizes from late July to October. Invest in higher quantities for small sizes.
  • Develop a contingency plan. When a hero runs low, its samples go to another in-stock hero, so the sampling plan keeps running.
  • Deep stock before every peak holiday or TikTok Shop campaign. Daze Off for the holidays and Valentine's, Cozy Crush and the Foldovers for spring, and new colors before each drop.

Ad spend averaged 21% of monthly GMV in 2026. ROI by campaign to be confirmed with the GMV Max export from Ads Manager.

Brand LIVEs build a regular audience on FBM's own account. Creator LIVEs put FBM in front of shoppers who already buy on LIVE. The Millie Mega Live is the moment that ties them together.

LIVE-only bundles and first looks at new colors and prints. Margins are thin, so bundles that raise order value work where deeper discounts aren't an option.

12 · Brand content

Brand content is an open lane for FBM

  • Post on the brand account every day. The brand account drove under 3% of 2026 sales. Short, shoppable videos of heroes and new colors can sell alongside creator videos.
