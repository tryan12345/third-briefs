1. Winning formula

Name the gift, and the toddler it is for, in your first line

The goal this season is more Tonies sales per video through Christmas. "Tonie box is the best gift for toddlers" returned $52.1 per 1,000 views. One buyer in five writes a review about a gift. Gift-givers need to hear who it is for before they hear what it is.

DoOpen with the child's age and the occasion: "If you're shopping for a two-year-old this Christmas."
Don'tOpen on the box with no reason to care.

Put the price, the deal or the free gift on screen

The single best Tonies video of last holiday season ($115.7 per 1,000 views) led with a Black Friday free gift in the caption. "Check your coupons before this Toniebox 2 bundle sells out" returned $89.3. "Click the cart and see if it still says free gift" returned $30.7.

DoShow the price and the free gift as text in the first three seconds, and say "tap the orange cart to check yours."
Don'tPromise a price or a gift you have not checked on the listing that day. Missing items, free gifts included, are the top reason for low reviews.

Play the audio on camera

Character drops and unboxings that play the Tonie out loud sell well. jills.tonie.clips' Judy Hopps unboxing is the top Tonies video of 2026 ($35.4K GMV). Her Baby Shark video opens on the runtime and song count.

DoPlace the Tonie and let the first song play for three to five seconds. Say how long it runs.
Don'tTalk over the audio or add trending music on top of it.

Show your child using it without help

Buyers write "easy for even our two-year-old to use" and "keeps him both off screens and entertained." Independence and screen-free time are the reasons parents give for buying.

DoFilm small hands placing the Tonie and squeezing the ear for volume.
Don'tStage it with an adult doing every step.

Use stock urgency only when it is true

"You're gonna regret waiting on this one" returned $55.5. "She sold out in 2 hours" returned $37.0. "These are going to SELL OUT fast" returned $36.2. All three ran when stock was low.

DoUse a restock or low-stock line when the listing shows it.
Don'tCall an in-stock item "almost gone."

2. Content angles

Angle 1

The toddler gift pick

Gap it answers: Grandparents and aunts know the child, not the system, and need one clear thing to buy.

Archetype: A5 Toddler Gift Video Creator.

How to run it: Name the age, show the starter set and what is in the box, play one Tonie, end on the price and the cart.

Angle 2

Toniebox 1, Toniebox 2 or Lite in 30 seconds

Gap it answers: The number one buyer question, which box to buy.

Archetype: A5 Toddler Gift Video Creator, or A2 Mom-Deals Live Seller as a live segment.

How to run it: Boxes side by side. Ages, charging, games and light ring. Finish on the bundle that is on deal.

Angle 3

The screen-free swap

Gap it answers: Parents who want less screen time but still need a quiet half hour.

Archetype: A4 Baby and Toddler Specialist.

How to run it: Car ride, nap or bedtime routine with the Toniebox doing the work. Ms. Rachel or My First Tonies as the hero.

Angle 4

The holiday Tonie for kids who already have a box

Gap it answers: Owners who want the next Tonie and a stocking stuffer at $19.99.

Archetype: A1 Tonies Superfan Host.

How to run it: "If you have a Toniebox, you need this Tonie for the holiday season." Play Holiday Songs 2 or the Grinch.

Angle 5

The live bundle drop

Gap it answers: Buyers who balk at $20 a Tonie and wait for a bundle price.

Archetype: A2 Mom-Deals Live Seller and A3 Big-Box Live Seller.

How to run it: A timed Toniebox 2 bundle in a live segment, with three to six pre-heat videos the day before.

How it works, in the order to say it

  • Set up the box once on Wi-Fi.
  • Place a Tonie on top and it starts playing.
  • Squeeze the ears for volume. Tap the side to skip.
  • Each Tonie downloads the first time, then works anywhere, including the car.
  • Toniebox 2 adds Tonieplay games, a light ring night light, a sleep timer and a sunrise alarm, and charges by USB-C.

Bundles buyers ask about

Multi-packs and bundles convert because buyers resist the per-Tonie price. Name the bundle saving out loud when one is live.

5. Objection questions to answer on camera

Answer these out loud. Do not wait for the comments.

  • Which box should I buy? Toniebox 2 for ages 1+ with games and a night light. Lite for a smaller, simpler box at a lower price.
  • Will it work with Tonies we already own? Yes. Both boxes play every Tonie.
  • Is $20 a Tonie worth it? Say how long yours runs and how often your child picks it.
  • Is it hard to set up? Show the setup. It takes one Wi-Fi connection.
  • Is it right for a one-year-old? Toniebox 2 and Lite are made for ages 1+. Start with My First Tonies or Ms. Rachel.
  • Will everything be in the box? Hold up every piece in the bundle you are selling.
