THIRD. · Makeup By Mario · Strategy

Makeup By Mario × TikTok Shop US

August closed at $148.6K. That is 32% below July and $55.5K short of the top 50 makeup brands. Creator video output is up about 8x since June. Sales per video are half the category median.

01 · The position

The shop sits one place outside the top 50

Makeup By Mario opened its TikTok Shop on November 6, 2025. It has $3.2M in lifetime GMV, 85,300 units sold and 60 SKUs. On the August ranking of makeup brand shops it would place 51st.

The finding

The program is growing activity and shrinking sales. The gap to the top 50 is conversion per video, plus a paid program that is carrying revenue the affiliate program should be earning.

Mario already posts more videos than the median top 50 brand. If those videos earned the median $221 each, August would have been about $314K.

A 2.7 GMV Max ROI implies roughly $49K to $60K of paid spend in August, about a third of shop GMV. Most of what the shop sold was bought.

02 · Trajectory

More videos every week, fewer sales

New linked videos rose from 55 in the week of June 13 to 432 in the week of August 29. Weekly GMV fell from $53K to $34K over the same stretch. Total shop GMV divided by new videos dropped from $964 to $79 per video.

Deals For You Days ran June 26 to 29 and produced $380K in four days. That is 42% of all GMV in the last 90 days, and June 28 alone did $171.9K. Outside that event the shop averaged about $8.0K a day in June, $4.8K a day in August and $4.6K a day so far in September.

03 · Diagnosis

What is working and what is not

Working

  • Buyers search for the brand. Product card sales (search and shop page) were 50% of GMV in the last 28 days.
  • There is a clear hero. Enhance & Pop Duo has $779K in lifetime GMV.
  • There is an entry product. Lip Lift Kit at $20 sold 4,710 units in 90 days.
  • Event days convert. Deals For You Days did $380K in four days.
  • Premium price holds. AOV is $38.61, about double the top 50 median.
  • Active creators post often. 0.66 videos per creator in August against a 0.30 median.
  • Mid-tier creators carry sales. Creators with 10K to 50K followers drove 62.5% of affiliate GMV.

Not working

  • Conversion per video. $114 against a $221 median. 43 of 49 ranked brands earn more per video.
  • The brand account does not sell. @makeupbymario has 2.03M followers. It posted 36 videos in 90 days with zero product links and $0 GMV. The top post did 1.4M plays. Soft Shimmer launch posts pointed to Sephora and makeupbymario.com.
  • Live has stalled. 2,271 affiliate lives in 90 days averaged $41 and one unit each. Live fell from 10.3% of GMV over 90 days to 1.5% over 28 days.
  • Sales rest on a few creators. Two creators drove 24.8% of 28-day affiliate GMV. The top 10 drove 44.7%.
  • Creator fit is loose. Beauty creators are 24% of active creators. 56 home-category creators produced $0.
  • Paid is carrying the shop. A 2.7 GMV Max ROI puts spend at roughly a third of GMV. At that level GMV Max claims most of shop revenue, so the affiliate program is earning less than the video count suggests. The brand's own ad posts run with no product link.
  • Catalog and ops. Soft Pop Blush Veil went from $58.5K over 90 days to $789 over 28. Creators note Enhance & Pop is back in stock. Shop response rate is 81%.

07 · Where Third starts

Required actions, sequenced to Black Friday

Black Friday and Cyber Monday land in late November. The shop has shown it can do $170K in a day when an event lines up. The plan builds to that window.

Weeks 1 to 2

Reset the roster

Map all 4,295 linked creators to the L-tier system on trailing 30-day GMV. Cut non-beauty and zero-sale creators. Protect the top 20 converters with retainers or rate increases. Confirm inventory on Enhance & Pop and Blush Veil. Lift response rate above 81%.

Weeks 2 to 4

Put the brand account on the shop

Link product on @makeupbymario posts. Spark the top affiliate videos through the brand account. Run one live hosted by Mario as a test. This depends on what the Sephora agreement allows.

Weeks 3 to 6

Rebuild the creative

Build the Informed Creative Brief from the top 75 selling videos and 1,500+ buyer comments. Current winners share a pattern: shade swatches with a bundle offer ("choose 2 and save" on Shimmer Shadow Duo), beauty creators with 10K to 50K followers, and over-50 creators. Kat James hit 8.3% engagement.

Weeks 4 to 8

Rebuild GMVMax from the actuals

Start from the August GMV Max cost figure. At a 2.7 blended ROI the return is close to shop GMV divided by ad spend, so the incremental return is lower. Fund proven videos, cut the rest, and stop running brand posts as ads without a product link. The goal is to shift revenue back to affiliate so spend can come down without GMV following it.

Weeks 6 to 11

Hit the Activation Target

Concentrate about 750 videos in the window ahead of Black Friday. Pair them with a TikTok Shop bundle offer. The house benchmark is 25 to 30% off MSRP, subject to Sephora terms. Run vetted affiliate lives and a brand live on event days.

09 · Risks and data notes

What could change this read

Sephora channel terms

Bundles, discounts and launch timing on TikTok Shop may be limited by the retail agreement. The brand account and offer recommendations depend on the answer.

Founder time

Brand-hosted live works best with Mario on camera. If he is unavailable, a house artist host is the fallback and should be tested before Black Friday.

Source differences

Marketplace datasets differ. Mario's August GMV reads $148.6K in one and $162.8K in another. Creator counts appear to be totals rather than August-only figures. GMV per video uses one dataset for every brand.

Paid figures are derived

Only the 2.7 GMV Max ROI is confirmed. Spend and shop efficiency are worked back from it using the relationship between GMV Max spend and reported revenue seen in ten account months. Both move if the actual cost line differs.
