03 · What happened

Sales follow hero products: a September sale, October sellouts, then new bundles and launches in November.

September

Why it grew

Bluey 3-Pack’s first full month ($139K), plus a fast bundle sale: Frozen, Spidey, Mystery Mailer and Bluey Toniebox 2 ($177K). Affiliate GMV: $366K.

October

Why it dipped

Those bundles sold out or stalled, falling from $177K to $11K. Sales shifted to $20 Tonies (Halloween, Ms. Rachel). Affiliate GMV: $295K, down 20%.

November

Why it grew: bundles on live

The new Pooh Toniebox 2 bundle (first sale Oct 28) sold $148K Nov 1–15, $80K of it on live. Shoppers are buying $210 bundles again, not just $20 items.

November

Why it grew: new launches

Danny Go! Pocket Tonies launched Nov 7 and sold $117K by Nov 15. Affiliate GMV hit $564K in 15 days, at $233 per video.

04 · The diagnosis

Demand is here. Speed is the gap: sample approvals, follow-ups and booked creator lives.

Working

Holiday demand

Affiliate GMV hit $564K Nov 1–15, 4x the same days in October. $233 per video, up from $150.

New launches

Danny Go! Pocket Tonies launched Nov 7: $117K by Nov 15. One demo video drove $46K.

Merchandising

Pooh Toniebox 2 bundle: $148K Nov 1–15, $80K of it on live. Bluey 3-Pack is still the top video figurine.

Not working

Sample approvals

526 requests wait in To Review (Nov 19). 466 are qualified, from 232 creators with $10K+ GMV in 30 days and a 70%+ post rate. 296 of them expire by Nov 22. November approved 266 boxes against a cap of 150. Approve figurines now. 75 boxes open Dec 1.

Upsell CRMs

337 creators got a Toniebox sample since July and 166 have sold Tonies, but no CRM follows up with the next product. Only the Content Pending and Overdue nudges are live. Add two figurine workflows (every box owner, and first sales) plus holiday broadcast CRMs.

Creator lives

There is no creator live program yet. Creator lives happen on their own: ~$53K of ~$327K live GMV in 28 days, half from two creators. Nothing is booked, there is no live commission, and taylorstinyworld sold only $391 after moving to a rate. Launch the program: December deals and live commission confirmed with Justin by Nov 20, then 3 to 5 creator lives a week, Dec 1 to 31.

10 · Risks

Four risks decide whether creator lives and samples deliver.

  1. Commission. Big-box live creators field holiday offers from larger toy brands. A flat 10% may not book them.
  2. Inventory. Booked live creators need in-stock bundles. Several Toniebox 1 starter sets show zero stock.
  3. Sample timing. Request to post takes about 12 days (2.5 to approve, 3.7 to deliver, 5 to post). Approvals after early December miss Christmas.
  4. Live creator concentration. Two creators drove $27K, about half of ~$53K creator live GMV in 28 days. taylorstinyworld fell from $26.6K to $391 and now charges a rate.
