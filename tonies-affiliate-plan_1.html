Figurine workflows and holiday broadcasts

Delivery builds two figurine workflows and the holiday broadcast CRMs below in Cruva. Copy is a starting draft for Delivery to finish.

Trigger: 1 to 2 orders from her Tonies videos.

“You made your first Tonies sales with your Toniebox. Nice work! Gift shopping peaks over the next three weeks, so keep posting. Here’s a target plan for [figurine] so your next video has something new to show.”

Offer: a figurine target plan, new launch or holiday title.

What to replicate

  • Lead with the price: a “glitch,” a stacked coupon, the price on screen. Only claim what is true on the day.
  • Name the gift out loud: their favorite person, the big gift, the stocking stuffer.
  • Show new launches with the audio playing. The top video, $47.2K, is an 11-second faceless demo of Danny Go! Pocket Tonies on a Toniebox.
  • Talk straight to Toniebox owners: “if you have a Toniebox, you need this.”
  • Tell viewers to tap the orange cart and check their coupon.

These hooks make up the one-pager sent with every approved sample and every workflow above. The full creator brief builds on them.
