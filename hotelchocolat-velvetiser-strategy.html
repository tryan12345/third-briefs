01 · The position

A proven product on a decaying shop

Hotel Chocolat USA has done $1,139,924 on TikTok Shop across 17,744 units. Roughly $921,000 of that is the Velvetiser family. The shop opened in October 2025, so the entire history is ten months long.

Last seven days: $450 on 9 units. That is a 98% drawdown from the peak on a product with no meaningful competition and 22,749 units of the hero SKU sitting in stock.

This is not a demand problem. It is a content-supply problem that was never rebuilt after the holiday.

The read

November 2025 was not a campaign. It was a seasonal accident that worked, and nobody built the machine to repeat it.

Zero GMV Max campaigns have ever run on this shop. The brand account has 5,861 followers and has produced $0 in shop-account GMV. 68% of the last 90 days of revenue came from product cards, which is residual search demand created by creators eight months ago.

Three things fall out of this that are not visible from the peak window alone.

The cold start took one month. August and September produced $537. October produced $18,171 on 193 videos from 68 creators, every one of them a first-time poster, with 162 samples delivered. Then November did $505K. There was no long ramp. There was a single intense seeding month immediately before the season.

Sampling front-loaded the peak. 162 samples went out in October. Only 65 went out across November and December combined. The seeding happens before the window, not during it.

The current number is not a floor. January through April ran at $21,153 a month. May through August is running at $4,955. The line is still falling. Treating $5K as a baseline understates how fast the residual demand is decaying.

The detail that reframes the plan

October 2025 was LIVE-driven. Of the $18,171 the shop did that month, $11,494 came from live streams. That is 63%. By November live had fallen to 4.7% of GMV and video carried the spike. The cold start and the harvest ran on two different mechanics, and only one of them was ever rebuilt.

Ninja CREAMi XL is the closest structural match. Same price band, same countertop footprint, same demo-driven creative, same razor-and-blade economics with the pint containers.

The correction

The category leader is more concentrated than the Velvetiser, not less. Ninja does not run a baseline. Ninja runs events.

Across 180 days the CREAMi XL did $3,067,796. $2,618,958 of it landed between June 12 and July 11. That is 85% of six months of revenue inside 30 days. Daily GMV went from a $1,695 average in the three weeks prior to a peak of $239,740 on June 28, a 141x move. By July 24 it was back to zero and has stayed there.

This matters because the obvious read on Hotel Chocolat is that they need to stop being a Q4 business. The comp data says otherwise. The best-performing appliance in this category is a four-week business with a long tail of product-card demand. The problem is not that the Velvetiser spikes. The problem is that it spiked once.

The gap in creators is real, and it is breadth rather than depth. CREAMi XL has 1,246 creators attached across its life. The Velvetiser has 227.

The second event

The Velvetiser makes cold chocolate. The brand has never sold it that way at scale.

Two videos posted in July 2026 led with iced chocolate. They converted at 12.6% and 15.2%, well above the shop's own baseline, on almost no views. A Summer Iced Chocolate bundle already exists in the catalog at $212 with 3,183 units in stock and 2 units sold. Ninja's whole year is a June event. This is the Velvetiser's version of one.

06 · The plan

Two events a year, seeded one month out, run on LIVE

November 13 to November 29 is when the machine sold last year. October is when the roster that sold it was built. The plan works backward from both.

Aug 11 – Sep 15 · Phase 1

Fix outreach and build the LIVE roster first

Outreach is running at a 0.61% reply rate on 27,575 DMs. Rewrite the offer around the appliance, not the chocolate. Recruit live sellers ahead of video creators, because October 2025 already proved live carries a cold start on this product and the comp set's biggest event ran on 2,450 live sessions. Target 15 active live sellers against the one the shop has today.

Aug 11 – Sep 30 · Phase 2

Run the iced chocolate event

Treat late August as a real event, not filler. Push the Summer Iced Chocolate bundle with a concentrated two-week creator push rather than a slow drip. The two July videos on this angle converted at 12.6% and 15.2%. This is the test of whether the product supports a second season, and the content it generates becomes ad inventory either way.

Oct 1 – Oct 31 · Phase 3

Repeat the October seeding sprint at 3x

October 2025 put 162 samples out and produced 68 first-time posters, and that was enough to trigger a $505K November. Run the same motion at three times the volume. Target 500 samples out and 200 first-time posters inside the month. This is the highest-leverage window in the plan and it has a proven conversion rate behind it.

Oct 1 – Oct 15 · Phase 4

Turn on GMV Max before the traffic arrives

House floor for a cold start is 50 affiliate videos a week. Current rate is 18.5. GMV Max readiness is roughly 50 videos per hero product or 600 total shop videos. Launch on Max Delivery in the first week of October so the campaign finishes learning before Black Friday. Creative-level ROI on this product has already printed 2.1 to 3.6 with no campaign behind it.

Oct 15 – Dec 24 · Phase 5

Harvest with the November creative

The hooks that sold the machine last year are documented and they are gifting hooks. Rebuild around them from October 15. Run the Velvetiser and the box collections as two separate creator tracks with separate briefs, because they sell to different buyers at different price points.

Jan 2027 onward · Phase 6

Convert the install base to refills

By January there should be several thousand machines placed through this shop. Refill GMV needs to be the January and February story. This is the only genuine baseline available on this product, and it is the thing the comp set does not have.

08 · Risks

What could break this

Product

Temperature is the dominant complaint in reviews. Three of the six sub-five-star reviews say the drink comes out warm rather than hot. One buyer said it barely gets warm. This is a review-score problem that gets worse as volume scales, and it needs a scripted answer on camera plus a real answer from the brand.

Sampling economics

The plan calls for 500 samples in October on a $212 machine. At full retail that is over $100,000 of product. Refundable samples or a paid seeding model is not optional here, it is what makes the October sprint affordable. This needs a decision from the brand before September.

Single-season concentration

81% of lifetime GMV sits in one product family and roughly 70% of it landed in eight weeks. The comp set is worse on this measure, not better, so the answer is not a year-round baseline. The answer is a second event and a refill business. Both are unproven on this shop and both start in this plan.

Data limits

Competitive data is capped at 180 days across both platforms, so no matched Q4 2025 comp window is available. The seasonality argument rests on Hotel Chocolat's own first-party history plus a summer event from the category leader. Re-run the comp read in November when a Q4 window becomes visible.
