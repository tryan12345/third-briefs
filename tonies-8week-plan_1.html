The headline

The SOW asks for at least one L3+ creator live a week. Tonies already has the creators to run five or six a week by Black Friday.

What this tells us

Viewers buy at almost the same rate. The fix is traffic: pre-heat videos before every show and bigger hosts for the peak weeks.

The standard every booked creator live follows

  • Two hours minimum, on a fixed slot that never overlaps a brand show.
  • Lead item: one Toniebox 2 bundle or the Toniebox Lite, pinned for the first 10 minutes and again at the close.
  • Second hero: a figurine 3- or 4-pack (Bluey, Pokémon or My First Tonies).
  • Three to five single Tonies played on air, new releases and holiday titles first.
  • One timed offer every 20 to 30 minutes, using only mechanics Tonies has approved.
  • Three to six pre-heat videos from the host in the 48 hours before the show.
  • A Third moderator in chat for the full show, answering age, setup and Toniebox 1 versus 2 questions.
  • Recap within 24 hours: GMV, units, viewer entries, GPM, top product.

What a big-box show is

A big-box host is one of the top Toys & Hobbies creator live sellers: $125K to $320K of Toys live GMV a month, 37 to 54 lives a month, and 10K to 20K viewer entries a show. They run daily multi-brand deal shows. Tonies does not get its own show. It gets a featured slot inside one.

The format

  • A 30 to 45 minute Tonies feature inside the host's regular show, in a peak week: Cyber Week, 12.12 or the ship-by week.
  • A show-only price or bundle that Tonies approves, plus the host kit.
  • Three to six pre-heat videos from the host, and a brand co-host on screen where Tonies agrees.
  • Paid as a flat fee plus commission, or a higher live-only rate. Tonies sets the budget.

What to replicate

  • Gift framing for a toddler, with the Toniebox 2 bundle in hand in the first second.
  • A real reason to buy now: the Black Friday price, a free gift or limited stock. Only claim what is true on the day.
  • Tell viewers to tap the cart and check their price or coupon.
  • New character releases unboxed with the audio playing on the box.
  • Toniebox 1 versus 2 for parents deciding which to buy.

The same hooks work as openers and resets in creator lives. A full creator brief can be built from these if needed.

Risks worth naming

Expiring requests

119 creators' sample requests expire on Nov 7. Requests not approved by then are lost and have to be re-invited.

Commission

Big-box hosts sell $100K or more a month on live. At 10%, they may not book, and lives fall back to the creators already selling Tonies.

Inventory

A lead bundle that sells out mid-show costs the slot. Confirm stock before each show.
