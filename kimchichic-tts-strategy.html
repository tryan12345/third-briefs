01 · The trajectory

March was the best month. GMV then settled near $250K as sampling slowed.

Feb to Apr averaged 1,460 approved samples and $465K a month. Jun to Oct averaged 595 samples and $251K. *Nov: $175K actual through Nov 17 (solid), projected $340K at the current pace (dashed), about $365K with a Black Friday lift.

02 · Why it happened

Three things drove the decline: higher hero prices, nothing new to sample, and capped ad reach.

Price

+$1.60

Puff Puff Pass average selling price, $15.49 in Jan to Mar, $17.07 in May to Sep

NPD

0

New products to sample. Approved samples fell 74%, March to October

Samples

3–4

Of the same items were sampled every month, so creators and content fatigued

Paid ads

10x

Ad ROI target most of the year. A high target limits how many new buyers ads reach

What we've changed

We've spent the last six months rebuilding Puff Puff Pass at its new price. It's starting to rebound, and it's still competitively priced against similar powders. Creators are getting products they haven't sampled before. Ads now run at a 7x target to reach more new buyers.

How it works

Each approved sample is worth about $153 a month in GMV. It brings about 2.8 videos (the Feb to Oct average; months ranged from 2.2 to 3.8), and each video adds about $55 a month. Adding 200 to 250 samples a month gets to $400K by March and $500K by May or June. Treat this as a guide, not a guarantee.

11 · Risks

Four things could change this read.

01

The equation.

Fit on nine months. February to April ran about $2 cheaper per unit and may have carried viral lift.

02

Sample inventory.

About 2,200 samples a month is about 26,000 units a year.

03

Hit dependence.

Only 12% of videos sell, and the top tenth earn almost all of it.

04

Order quality.

Half-empty jars and wrong items appear in 14% of low ratings.
