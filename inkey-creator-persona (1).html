THIRD.

Commerce creator persona

The INKEY List

Which creators actually convert the Neck Lift Stick and the Glycolic Acid Body Stick, how to tier them, and how many exist. Built from seller-side data only. November 2026.

Provisional. A full commerce creator persona compares INKEY’s sellers against competitor creators and competitor videos. Competitor creator lists were pulled for seven products. Competitor video data was not pulled, so format comparisons rest on INKEY’s own 2,286 videos from August 6 to November 3, 2026.

The archetypes and sizing hold. The format notes should be checked against competitor videos in the next version.

The pattern

Deal and lifestyle accounts with older audiences sell the neck stick

Of the 68 creators who have sold the Neck Lift Stick, 9% are classed as Beauty creators. The largest groups are Other at 58% and Shopping & Retail at 13%. The competing neck creams show the same split. The creators who sell it best talk to an audience aged 35 to 55+, either about their own neck or about a deal.

The two creators with the most INKEY GMV have stopped posting it. @jopickz ($113.0K lifetime) posted 81 INKEY videos in August, 19 in September and none since. @justjamiemichelle ($95.8K lifetime) went from 11 INKEY videos in August to 4 in October, while posting 166 videos for other brands in the last 28 days.

Sample screening is holding the bar. Posting after approval is the gap

5,760 sample requests came in over 90 days and 4,074 expired. 97% of the expired requests came through the open plan, from creators who found the product themselves. 94% of those creators had under $1K in 30-day GMV, and their median post rate was 31%. Approved creators had a median of $8.2K in 30-day GMV and an 86% post rate. Targeted invites were screened: 595 were rejected and 117 expired.

After approval, output drops. 479 approved samples produced 306 posted videos, 0.64 per sample, against a house benchmark of about 1.5. 67 approved creators are overdue. The pipeline itself is fast: a median of one day to ship, five days to deliver and two days to post.

Archetypes

Priority 1

A1. The Neck Storyteller

Follower ranges are used in place of L-tiers. 10k to 500k followers. Audience mostly women, 35 to 55+.

Buyer trigger served

Trigger 1, a change in her neck she can name, and trigger 2, loose skin after weight loss.

Format

Face on camera, 40 to 60 seconds. Opens on her own story: age, weight loss, what she tried before. Shows the stick going on in upward strokes. Replies to comments with new videos.

Proven in market

@annekoii, 458,731 followers, took $26.1K on the neck stick from seven videos, opening “as a person who’s lost 90 pounds.” @tinarene_1, 12,976 followers, sold 764 units from one 56-second video at 11.37 units per 1,000 views.

Priority 1

A2. The Deal Caller

5k to 135k followers. Audiences skew 45 to 55+.

Buyer trigger served

Trigger 3, a deal she might miss.

Format

10 to 40 seconds. Opens on the price or the coupon, product in hand. Low production, high cadence, often several products a day.

Proven in market

@socalshopdeals, 60,559 followers, sold 658 units from one video opening “Please don’t get scammed.” @gingermomchronicles sold 3.95 units per 1,000 views with “Check your coupons.” @killerdeals247 sold $283K across all products in the last 28 days.

Priority 1

A3. The Neck Category Switcher

20k to 180k followers. Proven sellers of $17 to $42 neck creams.

Buyer trigger served

Trigger 1, plus the stick format against jars.

Format

Already sells the category on video at volume. The pitch is a lower-priced product with a different format and twice the commission rate.

Recruiting note

@mirandacorneliusbeauty has taken $841.2K on Gopure’s neck cream and is already an INKEY affiliate at $9.6K lifetime. @affiliatetammytaylor has taken $1.17M on Dr.Melaxin’s neck set. @cakes.by.cynthia has taken $119.1K on Dr.Melaxin’s $17.60 neck cream from 125 videos.

Priority 2

A4. The Close-up Body Seller

7k to 140k followers. Mixed audiences, often younger.

Buyer trigger served

Trigger 4, a named body condition.

Format

Faceless, under 15 seconds. Skin texture in close-up, then the stick on the skin. Captions and hashtags carry the condition.

Proven in market

@jopickz has $113.0K in INKEY GMV, and one 10-second video took $16.3K. @mcjerbear sells with condition hashtags such as #bodyacne and #hidradenitissuppurativa. This archetype drove the September spike and has gone quiet since.

Priority 2

A5. The Spanish-Language Responder

10k to 40k followers.

Buyer trigger served

Trigger 4, especially dark underarms.

Format

Spanish-language replies to buyer comments, posted as new videos (“Respuesta a”). Each reply gives the next creator something to answer.

Proven in market

@zamb0806, 15,356 followers, has $15.9K in INKEY GMV, much of it from reply videos. Only five Spanish-language INKEY videos were posted in the window. Recruit eight to twelve together so the reply loop starts.

Sizing the hit list

Match method: current INKEY affiliates plus the top sellers of seven competing neck and body products. Gates: US account, $5K or more in 30-day GMV and four or more videos in the last 28 days. L-tier data was not available, so the gate replaces the L2 and L3 cut.

Commission and recruiting guidance

INKEY pays the most per sale in its price band, and discounts cut that by close to half

Dr.Melaxin Peel Shot$20.80 at 15% · $3.12

At the neck stick’s realized price of $9.49, 30% pays $2.85 per sale, close to what the competitor pays at full price.

Recruit on proof of selling this product shape, and review every sample request within 48 hours

  • Filter on GMV from neck or body competitors, not on follower count or the Beauty category.
  • Favor creators who talk to camera. Face-on-camera videos earned $21.97 per 1,000 views against $7.19 for faceless ones.
  • Affiliate coordinators review the open-plan queue daily against a written bar: $5K+ in 30-day GMV, a 70%+ post rate and a fit with the neck or body buyer. Decline the rest with the existing template.
  • Keep the open plan to the two sticks, with refundable samples on. Every other product goes to targeted invites.
  • Offer the two quiet top affiliates a direct reactivation deal: @justjamiemichelle on the neck stick, which fits her 45 to 55+ audience, and @jopickz on the Body Stick.
  • Keep any 35% to 40% rate for creators who commit to a posting cadence on one product.
  • Follow up the 67 approved creators who are overdue on their video, and report GMV-producing creator rate and GMV per sample sent.
