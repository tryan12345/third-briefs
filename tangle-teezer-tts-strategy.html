The bet

Shoppers ask one question before buying a detangling brush: which one is right for my hair? Tangle Teezer's range answers it. Creators with that hair type show the answer on camera.

The 2024 shop was run as a social channel. We plan from a clean start: nine products, Amazon fulfillment, and a shop run for sales.

The brand to copy: Wet Brush

An older detangler brand that joined TikTok Shop late, in August 2025. It now sells about $9,900 a week. Creators drive 77% of sales, and it pays to boost the creator videos that already sell.

Shoppers here will pay $13 to $31 for a brush. Price is not what holds a brand back.

Send samples in December so videos post on launch day

Each creator sample turns into about 1.5 videos, two to six weeks after it arrives. Samples sent in January mean videos in mid-February.

07 · Amazon lift

We report one number: extra Amazon sales for every $1 sold on TikTok

Amazon lift on the 9 TikTok products
minus Amazon lift on products not on TikTok
divided by TikTok Shop sales

Subtracting the products that are not on TikTok removes seasonality and Amazon promotions. We also check whether Amazon searches jump in the days after a TikTok video takes off.

The baseline has to start in November, before launch, or there is nothing to compare against.

09 · Risks

What could slow this down

No December samples

The whole plan shifts about six weeks later.

Slow Amazon shipping

Late orders hurt the shop's ranking. We test it in December.

Retail price clashes

TikTok offers have to respect prices at Ulta, Amazon, Target and CVS. Revolve already sells Tangle Teezer on TikTok.

New brand account

It starts with zero followers, so creators carry the first six months.
