Five creators who sell this mop

Built from seller-side data: 8,816 creators with activity in the 90 days ending October 14, 2026, $514,113 in creator-attributed GMV, and the quarter's 80 top-selling videos.

The pattern

Mid-tier creators carry this shop. Accounts with 10k to 50k followers are 43% of sellers and 40% of creator GMV. Accounts over 100k are 8% of sellers and 38% of GMV, so the big accounts still matter, and susanluckhardt proved the ceiling at $46,215 this quarter. Audience beats follower count: every converting profile skews 66% to 86% female, ages 25 to 54.

Format predicts conversion more than reach. Only 5.6% of the quarter's 4,402 new videos drove tracked sales, and the ones that did show the water, name the product, and frame an upgrade. Three of the shop's top ten sellers this quarter sold through live rooms only. The standing rule holds: no archetype match, no sample.

Archetype 1

The Clean-With-Me Mom

10k-150k followersVideoPOV reset content

She films her own house getting clean: porch transformations, Sunday resets, postpartum catch-up days. The mop sits inside content her audience already watches. Her videos run 15 to 95 seconds and the water reveal is the payoff shot.

Buyer trigger she hits: upgrade regret and whole-home range. Her "I finally retired my old bucket" framing converts replacement buyers.

Proven this quarter: brittany_qz ($10,636), thems.ligon, sydney.shops, b_saylor, kaley_silvaa.

Archetype 2

The Deal Curator

Any tierVideo, high volumeDeal storefronts

Posts daily, often several times, around platform deal events and price drops. Runs fast demos with the price on screen. One curator account placed four videos in the quarter's top thirty. This archetype converts hardest inside deal windows, which produced every $13K-plus day this quarter.

Buyer trigger she hits: deal windows and price justification. She is the distribution plan for Q4 events.

Proven this quarter: jessicadeals ($16,787), tntdealss, carls_deals, liz_the_clearancequeen, dealzfromtiktok.

Archetype 3

The Multilingual Household Voice

5k-750k followersVideoSpanish first, Arabic and Haitian Creole proven

She sells home pride in her own language. Spanish-language creators converted across every product this quarter, including the single best-reaching video in the window, an organic Spanish post that moved 104 sponge units. Arabic and Haitian Creole accounts also placed in the top sellers. This is a wider archetype than the July build gave it credit for.

Buyer trigger she hits: hands never touch dirty water. It is the loudest trigger in Spanish-language reviews, in her audience's own words.

Proven this quarter: lily_moradel ($11,382), yolilucas123, harimizzy, jonathan_recomienda, its.massielll, lindakitchen8, chocolatetea24, anapatriciatv.

Archetype 4

The Live Demo Host

5k-120k followersLiveMulti-product rooms, repeat placement

She runs long bazaar rooms and mops the floor on camera between other products. Live took 14.4% of shop GMV this quarter with no dedicated program, and three live-only sellers sit in the shop's top ten. One host ran five of the quarter's top ten sessions. She is where the live program starts. Her rooms already convert, and dedicated O-Cedar hours are the next step.

Buyer trigger she hits: the dirty-water reveal, performed live. The tank fills with brown water in real time and the cart opens.

Proven this quarter: things_athome ($12,754), winpom16 ($11,649), karenshopstts ($10,862), mrs.maker.of.the.home, shoppingwithlway.

Archetype 5

The Lineup Explainer

Any tierVideoComparison format

New this refresh. She answers the question the listings don't: which O-Cedar is which. A four-minute side-by-side of the three mops earned $2,393 this quarter, and "there is a clear winner" framing sold across accounts. With three heroes at three prices and a one-star record full of wrong-item confusion, this archetype protects conversion and cuts returns.

Buyer trigger she hits: the number one buyer question, "which mop am I actually getting?"

Proven this quarter: rachelwarren66 ($2,393 on one comparison), tntdealss comparison formats.
